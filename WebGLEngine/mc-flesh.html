Marching-cubes flesh — a surface graded against the field it came from

A smooth organic surface extracted from a metaball field we know exactly, by marching tetrahedra, shaded with normals taken straight from the analytic gradient so there is no faceting. Runs physics/mesh/marchingCubes.js. The mesh is watertight (a closed manifold), its vertices sit on the isosurface because the crossing is interpolated, and the volume it encloses converges to the analytic value — the same honest move as the other benchmarks, here for a mesh: define the truth, build the thing, grade it against the truth.
