HMC leapfrog — fleet kernel bench

4096 chains × 24 leapfrog steps over the standard 2D Gaussian fixture. The GPU proposes endpoints; the CPU recomputes every one in f64 and grades against the earned tolerance (5e-5, floor measured 2.6e-6 by tools/roundhouse/hmcGpu-selfcheck.mjs). A faster kernel that disagrees is REJECTED — its time is irrelevant. This page needs WebGPU: it is a rig instrument, not a sandbox one.
