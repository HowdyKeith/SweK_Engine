Ledger reports

A read-only view of the performance and persistence ledgers, for human eyes. This page adjudicates nothing — it renders whatever the target host reported. The verdicts it shows were computed on that host; this is a viewer, not a grader.

A different host must allow cross-origin reads (CORS) for this to work from the browser; a same-origin (blank) target always works. The page fetches <target>/perf and <target>/fingerprint and shows exactly what they return, labelled with their origin.
