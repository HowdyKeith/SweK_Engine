Paramecium — a reflex that beats a learner

A single creature in a chemical gradient. It gets 75 decisions and 26 directions on a Fibonacci sphere. A mold reflex — sample the neighbouring headings, go toward the strongest smell — is raced against UCB1, a real reinforcement learner. This page runs simulation/life/paramecium.js and brain/bench/bandit.mjs directly: the same modules the gate runs, not a recording.

Why the learner loses. UCB1 assumes its arms are stationary — that pulling arm 3 means the same thing every time. In a moving body every arm changes every step: "north" from here is not "north" from three metres away. Its memory is its handicap. With 26 arms and 75 decisions it cannot even try each arm three times.
What this page will not tell you. One peak, one start, one gravity. The mold's advantage here is a fact about this arena, not a law. The kill condition is on predictions.html.
