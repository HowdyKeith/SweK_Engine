Trace Truth — SweK as a 4D ground-truth generator

The inversion of a method like Trace Anything. That kind of model is handed a video and must estimate where every point went in 3D. SweK is a simulation — it knows. It runs tools/groundtruth/traceField.js: a deterministic moving scene whose every 3D track is exact, projected to the 2D observations an estimator would see. Below: the observations on the left, the true tracks on the right, and a naive estimator graded against SweK's exact answer. The observation is a ray — depth is genuinely lost — so recovering 3D is real inference, and the ground truth is what makes it gradeable.

What the estimator sees — 2D observations (with trails)

bright = visible; hollow = hidden behind a solid this frame. Depth is not given — two points on the same ray look identical.

The actual rendered frame — depth image the estimator consumes (z-buffered)

brighter = nearer. Points a nearer splat covers are gone from this frame — point-vs-point self-occlusion, straight from the depth buffer.

The estimator's grade vs SweK's exact 3D truth

EPE = mean 3D end-point error against the true track. The "cheat" estimator uses the true depth and scores ~0 — proof the pipeline is exact; the honest estimators must guess depth and pay for it.
