The sea is the physics surface a hull would float on (render/seaThree.mjs); subs are tinted toward the water colour by depth, and each sub’s tail planes and rudder follow its pilot’s command. A sub in an event shows its tank as a gauge on the hull, sheds bubbles when it blows, and a released weight falls to the floor.

Compass dropout

Both subs lose the compass for 16 s in every 40 (after 10 s of it working). Green is the true heading, amber is what the pilot is shown. The plain autopilot is shown the last good heading and drifts off it; the navigator integrates the hull’s own turn rate and keeps it. Its rate gyro is exact here, so this is the best a navigator can do.
