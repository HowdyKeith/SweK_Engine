The verdict carries, the trade does not

The previous round measured three repairs for an undetermined gradient in 2D and predicted that the verdicts would carry to three dimensions — the key is dimension-free — while the trade would not, because 6n deficient cells against a widened stencil on every cell is a different sum from two cells against the same widening. Both halves hold.
And one result is sharper in 3D than it was in 2D. A false zero-Neumann condition reads 1.134 here against the refusal's 1.000 — worse than doing nothing. In 2D it read 0.547 against 1.000 and looked better than doing nothing. The 2D number was flattering by accident of dimension, and a reader who had only seen it would have drawn a milder conclusion than the truth. That is the argument for running the second dimension rather than asserting the verdict carries.

1. The three repairs in 3D

Rank is not correctness, in either dimension: the Neumann row closes the deficiency, the matrix is invertible, and the answer is wrong. Supplied with the honest normal derivative the same machinery is exact — same rank, same rows, only the right-hand side differs. The mechanism is sound; the assertion is what is false.

2. Where the dimensions part company

The widening is ~4× more expensive in 3D — 14.8× the rows against 2D's 3.9× — because a tet has four vertices and each is shared by many more cells, so the neighbourhood doesn't gain a dimension, it multiplies.
But per repaired cell it is cheaper in 3D, which is the opposite of the obvious reading. The deficient set grew 24× while the stencil grew 4×. Two true numbers answering different questions: the total extra work is 16× higher in 3D, and the cost per defect fixed is 1.5× lower. Which matters depends on whether the budget is the solver's total or the engineer's patience — and quoting one as "the cost" is the shape this arc keeps finding.
Dirichlet meanwhile costs almost nothing in either dimension and is exact. So where the data exists the choice is not close — and the vertex stencil's whole appeal is that it works where the data does not. Graded by physics/mesh/rankRepair3-selfcheck.mjs from gates.html.
