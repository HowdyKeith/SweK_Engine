Denoiser

A learned denoiser for the path tracer, on the device — ← back · Path Tracer

Trained on two families of scenes, R and C, and this page renders only those. Round 11 found it beats the hand-written filter on new scenes of both. Across eleven pre-registered rounds, no network in the arc ever beat the filter on a family it was not trained on, in either direction. Nothing here says it works on any other scene — the path tracer's own scenes included.

idle

The numbers are relMSE against the reference (lower is better): the arc's own metric, mean of (y - r)2 / (r2 + 0.01). The reference here is a display, rendered at the samples chosen above; the arc's references were 1024 samples a pixel. The network is round 11's seed 1, loaded from render/denoise-net-r11.json; the filter is the joint bilateral filter tuned on the same 192 training scenes. Seeds that belong to the arc's dataset are refused: this page never renders a scene the network was trained or tested on. On the device the network runs as WebGPU compute (render/denoiseDevice.mjs); without WebGPU it runs on the CPU, and the caption says which.
