Interferometer — metrology of a surface we know exactly

A phase-shifting interferometer, simulated. We define a metaball surface we know to the bit, imprint its height as optical phase, and record the four-step fringe pattern a real instrument would. Then we recover the height back from the fringes alone — the reconstruction never sees the truth — and grade it against the surface we own. Runs physics/optics/interferometer.js. The instrument is simulated; the optics is real; the answer is checkable.
