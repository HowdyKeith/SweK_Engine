Which backprojector? — watch the shipped default turn round

Both panels below run the SAME iteration — x ← x + step·B(b − Ax), the step DERIVED per operator by power iteration — and differ only in B. Left uses backProject, the operator sirt.mjs has shipped since v3613. Right uses matchedBackProject, the transpose of radon built by reversing its innermost assignment (v3616: dot-product defect 8.304e-14 against backProject's 2.751e+1). Let it run and watch the residual readouts. The left panel sharpens, reaches a best point, and then walks away from the data — residual rising, correlation falling — because an unmatched pair is not a descent method. The right one keeps descending. Measured at THIS page's settings: at 30 angles backProject bottoms out around iteration 600 (residual 6.62, correlation 0.9862) and is back to 6.84 / 0.9850 by 1200, while the matched adjoint goes 5.44 → 2.34 with correlation rising to 0.9835. At 90 angles the same shape arrives later — backProject bottoms near iteration 900 (12.1 / 0.9972) and rises to 12.6 / 0.9966, while the matched one falls 13.0 → 3.97 and reaches 0.9992. Press 30 to see it plainly; at 90 the pixels barely move and the numbers carry it.

A caution about a claim I got wrong on this page's first draft: v3616 measured, at 16 angles on a different phantom, that the matched operator settled at a lower correlation than backProject touched at its best moment — converging on the objective is not the same as being closest to the truth. It holds at 30 angles here and REVERSES at 90. At 30 the matched operator wins the residual (2.34 vs 6.84) and loses the correlation (0.9835 vs 0.9850); at 90 it wins both (3.97 / 0.9992 against 12.6 / 0.9966). So the correlation comparison FLIPS with angle count while the residual comparison does not — the wrinkle is an effect of SPARSE angles, not a general law. My first draft of this paragraph claimed the matched operator won both at 30, and the gate caught it by re-deriving these numbers from this page's own constants, which is why they are quoted with their settings.
