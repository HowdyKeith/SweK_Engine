| `splat-gpu-sort` | Sort splats back to front on the graphics card instead of the CPU | `src/scene/graphics/radix-sort/compute-radix-sort-multipass.js` and its three WGSL chunks (`src/scene/shader-lib/wgsl/chunks/radix-sort/`) | **SORT DONE 2026-11-21:** `render/splatSortGPU.mjs` (PlayCanvas's 4-bit multipass radix sort on raw WebGPU, plus SweK's depth keys on the card). `tools/ship/splatSortGPU-selfcheck.mjs` holds it to the CPU sort: the same permutation, ties included, from 1 to 100,000 keys. **DRAWN FROM 2026-11-21:** `render/splatRendererGPU.mjs` draws straight from the sorted index buffer (engine/SplatRenderer.js's math in WGSL, premultiplied blend), held by `tools/ship/splatRendererGPU-selfcheck.mjs` to a CPU evaluation of the same math (mean 0.45/255 a channel) and to the order flipping when the camera crosses. **ON A PAGE 2026-11-21:** `splat-gpu.html` (WebGPU, WebGL2 fallback, drop a .ply or .splat), held by `tools/ship/splatGpuPage-selfcheck.mjs`: the two backends draw the same picture (mean 0.05/255 on SwiftShader).
