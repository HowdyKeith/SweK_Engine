 SweK iOS Device Tools

adb, for iOS. doronz88/pymobiledevice3 (GPL-3.0) speaks the protocols an iPhone already speaks to a trusted computer — usbmux, lockdown, AFC. No jailbreak, and nothing is installed on the phone: it needs a device paired the ordinary way, by tapping Trust This Computer.

⚠️ No iPhone has ever been attached to this bridge.
The box it was written on has no USB device and no pymobiledevice3 installed. Detection, the allowlist and every refusal are gated and run — those are the paths that must behave with nothing plugged in, which is this box's actual state. Everything past “the CLI answered” is unverified and marked below.
This does not promote the iOS peer, and saying so is the point. An iPhone still cannot run node or host ai-bridge/server.js, so ios-peer.html's “a subset in Safari” stands and the peer ladder is unchanged. What changes is the host's grip on the phone: a management channel iOS has never had here.
