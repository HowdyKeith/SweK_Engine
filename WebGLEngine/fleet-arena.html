Fleet Agent — a learned scheduler that routes by capability

Runs brain/agent/fleet.js, the module the gate runs. A task stream is routed across brains with different per-kind speeds — one fast at nav, another at render. Four schedulers compete for the lowest makespan (when the last brain finishes): random, round-robin, a greedy load-balancer, and a learned router that learns each brain's speed per kind from the times it sees and sends work to whoever finishes it soonest.
