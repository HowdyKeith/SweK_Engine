A counting law grades a force field

Bonds, angles, dihedrals and improper were each gated separately when they were built. Normal modes grade them together, through a quantity none of them computes — the second derivative of the potential — and every key here is a fact about space rather than a number anybody typed.
A non-linear molecule of N atoms has exactly 3N−6 vibrations and six zero modes (three translations, three rotations). A linear one has 3N−5 and five, because rotation about the axis moves nothing. Those zeros must come out at machine zero — so one check grades every force term at once.

1. The count

Every fixture is an exact stationary point by construction — every bond at r0, every angle at cos0, so each term sits at its own minimum and the total force is zero with no optimiser tolerance anywhere in the key.

2. Two routes sharing no code

The roundhouse's bond mode reaches this number by integrating the molecule and counting oscillations. This reaches it by a numerical second derivative and an eigenvalue. No shared code between the two paths, and they agree to 3×10−12.

3. What the count found in shipped code

The count is right; the potential is not. V = ½k(cos t − cos t₀)² with cos t₀ = −1 gives (ε²/2)² near straight — quartic, so the second derivative is exactly zero and the bending force constant vanishes. A linear molecule built with this term has no restoring force against bending, and its degenerate bend is missing.
angles.js says why it is cosine-harmonic — avoiding acos to keep cross-arch determinism, a real reason and a good one — and never mentions that the choice degenerates at 180°. Not a bug: a documented trade with one end undocumented, and a counting law from geometry is what exposed it in a term that had already passed its own gate. Nothing was changed in angles.js; the limit is now written down.

4. The load-bearing negative

Translations are zero modes at any geometry; rotations only at a stationary point. The potential cannot see where the molecule is, but it can see how it is turned unless the forces vanish. So "I got six zeros" is evidence the geometry is at a minimum — and a fixture that never checked its own equilibrium could report the wrong count and look right.
