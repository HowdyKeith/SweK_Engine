Prices your own roundhouse traffic with frugon (MIT, local, zero network calls in analyze mode), then orders escalateRoute's chain by verified-per-dollar — using the gate result frugon can't see. Frugon infers whether a cheaper model would have sufficed; escalateRoute ran a real gate and knows.
Per-call cost from frugon's report, as JSON. Typed, not scraped — frugon prints a box-drawing TUI, and a scraper for it would be an undeclared dependency on someone else's formatting that breaks silently on their next release while still returning a confident number.
