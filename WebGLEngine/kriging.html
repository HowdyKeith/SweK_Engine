variance — dark is confident, bright is not. It is exactly zero at every sample, and grows with distance from all of them.
Kriging is the best linear unbiased estimator: the weights sum to exactly 1 (unbiased), and of every weight set that does, this one gives the smallest variance (best). The variogram is a modelling choice — it is not derived from the samples, and changing it changes the answer.
