Agent Arena — a learner that beats its baselines

Not a mockup: this page runs brain/agent/arena.js — the exact module the gate runs. A deterministic engagement (an agent, a goal, threats on patrol) raced by four policies: random, a greedy dash, a reactive reflex, and a learned bandit that picks among four tactics (rush / flank / hold / flow, the GPU brain solved field) and updates its values from reward. Everything below is read straight out of the run — the Q-weights, the picks, the per-arm reward — not animated stand-ins. The kill condition is on predictions.html.
