Economy Probe — spaceprojectsim

Pause their sim, plant a known supply delta, step exactly one tick, and read whether the ship's decision changed. A counterfactual, not an inference. Most ships cannot be measured: their plan_goal is a priority waterfall, so any ship resting, refuelling, selling or retrofitting never reaches the scored band at all.
location commodity delta ship
Delta is clamped to ±5000 and the step to 50 ticks. The sim is left PAUSED afterwards on purpose — auto-resuming would destroy the state you just perturbed.
Sweep multiplicatively, not additively. Their score is ln(profit) × (1 + prefer_goals). Every doubling shifts it by exactly ln2 regardless of base, but a fixed +100 is a 0.693 shift from a base of 100 and only 0.095 from 1000. An additive sweep tests a moving target and reads the falloff as planner insensitivity when it is just arithmetic.
