LQR guarantees |1 + L(jω)| ≥ 1 at every frequency, so the Nyquist plot cannot enter the unit disc around −1 — which means no amount of extra gain can destabilise this loop. Wind κ up to 1000 and the pole stays up. Wind it down and it falls at about 0.36; the guarantee is 0.5, and a guarantee is a lower bound.
