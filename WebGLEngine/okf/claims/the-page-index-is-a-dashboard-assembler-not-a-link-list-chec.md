# The page index is a dashboard-assembler, not a link list -- check pages and watch them live side by side

- **Status:** settled  
- **Since:** v2746

## Prediction

The engine has grown to hundreds of pages, and a flat list of links is close to useless at that size. So the index groups every page and makes it searchable, and then does the thing a list cannot: you check any set of pages and it assembles them into a live dashboard of tiles, each page running in its own frame so you can watch several at once. The nuance is that pages are not uniform -- some are full scenes, some are panels -- and tiling them in frames lets each keep its own document and its own loops without stepping on the others, which is also why the swap-away bug that hit the brain pages cannot happen here.

## Why

page-index.html holds an embedded catalog of every page with its title and description, grouped by kind. A search filters live; a checkbox adds a page to the dashboard; the dashboard renders the checked pages as lazy-loaded iframe tiles and remembers the selection across visits. Because each tile is an isolated document, a heavy WebGL page in one tile does not touch another.

## Measured

The index lists 254 pages across eleven groups -- console, brain, ground truth, cosmic, physics, fluid, blobs, voxel, science, endless sky, and the rest -- generated by scanning the pages for their titles and first descriptions. The dashboard assembles and clears, and the selection survives a reload.

## Kill condition

Open page-index.html: search should filter instantly, checking pages should assemble live tiles, and the set should persist. HONEST SCOPE: the catalog is generated from the pages as they are now; a page added later needs a re-scan to appear. The tiles are whole pages in frames, not extracted mini-panels -- true panel extraction, pulling just the live-mind or a single gauge out of a page, is a further step.
