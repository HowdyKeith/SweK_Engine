Self-diffusion, two routes

The fourth pair on the consistency board and the first with no closed form on either side: a Lennard-Jones D at one state point is not something algebra hands you, so two mechanisms are the only check there is. Einstein integrates displacement over long times; Green-Kubo correlates velocities over short ones. They fail in opposite directions, so between them they say which problem you have.
