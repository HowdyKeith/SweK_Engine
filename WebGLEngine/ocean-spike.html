Ocean spike — web-ocean in a frame

Another project's WebGPU ocean (FFT waves, whitecaps, foam, a physically based sky), run as it is, so its cost on this GPU is measured before any of it is taken into the engine. It is its own canvas with its own camera: nothing of SweK's is drawn into it, nothing in it hides a SweK object, and no physics reads it. Click the sea to look around (Esc releases), WASD to move, H for its settings. Back to the server page.
