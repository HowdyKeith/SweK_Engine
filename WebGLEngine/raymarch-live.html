Live region ray march -- one texture over a window of chunks

The ray-march pass takes a single volume and a world does not fit in one 3D texture. This is the answer that closes the v2783 note: a 4x4 window of 16x64x16 chunks stitched into one 64x64x64 R8UI texture that FOLLOWS THE CAMERA, derived from the canonical RLE chunks, re-derived only when something it depends on moves.
It does not composite with the raster pass. There is no depth exchange between the marcher and the meshed world, so this draws the region on its own. That is a separate piece of work with its own answer key, and saying so here is cheaper than someone discovering it later.

The two checks, kept apart on purpose

Signature is cheap and runs every frame: window origin plus each chunk's fingerprint. It answers "has anything we track changed" and is not a correctness claim. verify() walks every voxel against the chunks and names the first divergence, the chunk that owns it, and the local coordinate inside that chunk.
