Steve Swerling

For my father, Steve Swerling

This project is dedicated to the memory of my father, Steve Swerling — one of the founding engineers of Mentor Graphics.

In 1981, he and a small team bet a company on an idea. Rather than build their own computers, they would build software for machines that could each stand alone or link together as peers — sharing compute directly, with no mainframe bottleneck in the middle. They chose the Apollo workstation, and Mentor Graphics went on to become one of the giants of electronic design automation: the software the world still uses to design the chips inside nearly everything.

Some forty years later, without knowing that history, I spent nineteen hundred-odd iterations building my own small version of the same idea — a fabric of ordinary machines, each running the whole system, linked together as peers. It turns out it runs in the family.

Thanks, Dad. This one's yours too.
