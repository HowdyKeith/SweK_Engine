EV cosmic map — a galaxy you can verify

Two galaxies, and only one of them is fully connected. A star map for the port, with the verification a map usually lacks. Runs physics/galaxy/cosmicMap.js. The galaxy is proven fully connected (every system reachable), its jump links proven two-way, and any route you plot is the shortest one — breadth-first, minimal jumps, every hop a real link. Click two systems to route between them; the path drawn is guaranteed the fewest jumps.

THE LAB swaps the seeded galaxy for the real one: every system is a device and every jump link is a measured relation that says which kind it is — substrate (the binds import a physics module in common), coupling (two observables agree to ten digits), room (the instrument pages sit in the same panel). It is an archipelago, and that is the finding rather than a fault: on the substrate axis alone 53 of 67 devices have no navigable neighbour at all. Routing to an island refuses instead of inventing a hop. Built by tools/roundhouse/labGalaxy.mjs --write; no clock anywhere in it, so this map is the same on a downloaded build as on the live hub.
