Predictive flow — anticipating a threat's next move

The threat maneuvers; the shooter fires where it will be, not where it is. A physics/predict/predict.js quadratic predictor reads the threat's acceleration off its recent track and marks its next position, and a firing lead solves the intercept -- a quadratic in time, one square root -- so the shot and the threat arrive together. The leading shot connects; the no-lead shot aimed at the current position sails behind. Graded against the future the threat actually reaches; the intercept solve is bit-identical across the fleet.
