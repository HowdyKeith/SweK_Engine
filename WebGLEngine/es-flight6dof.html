Every fruit-fly pilot is the same network, wired like a fly's Giant Fiber circuit, with the autopilot's contract (pitch, roll, yaw and throttle from the pointing errors, the rates and the air data); a choice picks which WEIGHTS fly it. The PD autopilot is not a network, and the hand weights are that autopilot written through the network (they fly the same flight); the shipped pilot is the same network with weights trained on the job the number under each name is graded on. The GPU brain is a DIFFERENT network (two dense layers, 1,796 weights) trained on the same job and run on a WebGPU device. A change applies at once, mid-flight; Start also respawns. You: S/↓ pull back (nose up), W/↑ push, A/← roll left, D/→ roll right, Q/E rudder, R/F throttle up/down; neutral is trimmed for your airspeed.
real physics: gravity, a stalling wing, a ground that ends the flight — drag to turn the camera, scroll to zoom, double-click to reset (chase and orbit cameras both)
