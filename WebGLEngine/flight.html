What it is

Two fleets pick targets with a tactics policy, fly with the engine's flight model, and shove each other around as Box3D bodies while shots resolve hits. It runs today in a fast 2D top-down view, and now in a WebGPU 3D view rendering the exact same simulation — render swapped, sim untouched. Because Box3D is deterministic across machines, this sandbox can eventually run in lockstep across the whole fleet.

The road ahead

  • ✓ Player ship — take the stick in Flight GPU: hit Fly, then A/D + W + Space to dogfight among the Brain's pilots.
  • ✓ Weapon FX — muzzle flashes, impact & kill bursts, and engine glow are in Flight GPU. Next: a cockpit view.
