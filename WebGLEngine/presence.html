Devices

Nothing presence-related is in Home Assistant yet. For "is someone home", add a device_tracker — the HA Companion app on each phone (GPS + zones) is the most reliable, or router/ping/Bluetooth trackers — then create person entities. For room occupancy, add motion or mmWave presence sensors (e.g. Aqara FP2, ESPresence, motion sensors) — they show up as binary_sensor with device_class occupancy/presence/motion.
