Splat Lab

3D Gaussian splatting graded against closed forms. This is not a viewer and loads no capture: a splat cloud is supposed to differ from the surface it approximates, so the render is not gradeable — but the projection and compositing arithmetic have exact answers, and those are what is measured here.

The projected splat

A 3D Gaussian projected through the perspective Jacobian. Move it off-axis and watch the shear tilt it — that term is exactly zero on the view axis, which is why an implementation that omits it looks perfect until something leaves the middle of the screen.

Exact keys

Each row is a closed form the code is never told. Computed live in your browser.

Order dependence — the load-bearing negative

Compositing is a product of transmittances for alpha and a weighted sum for colour. So the accumulated alpha cannot care about order, and the colour must. A renderer whose depth sort does nothing passes the first test and fails the second.
