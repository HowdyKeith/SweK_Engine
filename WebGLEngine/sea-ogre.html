◆ Sea OGRE vs Subs

One OGRE, four hit zones, a headquarters to hold. The tubes and the sonar are what let it shoot; the cannon shells the headquarters while it is surfaced; the engine is its speed. A torpedo detonates on the nearest zone and hurts the hull for half of it. Pick up to four defenders (each a tactician plus a pilot), choose a seed and watch, or run 12 seeds for the defence rate. The fight is a replay you can watch from outside, in plan, or from the bow of any defender (first person, no controls: it is a recording). The tank is sized to hold the whole fight (at least 1500 x 1000 m) and the boats are drawn three times life size.
