Sphere impostor — drag the sun

Both discs take the same light. Watch the boundary.
flat discone constant normal. Its boundary is straight and slides across.
sphere impostorN = vec3(p, sqrt(1 − p·p)). Its terminator bows, and goes crescent at grazing.
sun

Why two of them. A lit sphere and a shaded disc look the same in a still frame — both are bright on one side. The flat disc is drawn here on purpose, as the failing case: if the right-hand boundary ever looks like the left-hand one, the impostor normal is not reaching the fragment.

What to look for. Drag the sun toward the rim. The flat boundary stays a straight line; the sphere's narrows to a crescent. Push past the edge and the light clamps edge-on, which is where the two differ most.
