🔔 Doorbell fan-out
One trigger, many reactions. Fire it from a phone whenever Blink reports a doorbell/motion event — MacroDroid/Tasker → HTTP GET /doorbell/ring. It announces on the avatar (quip + TTS + HA speaker), pops the Blink app on the Android TV Device, and can flash a light. Blink stays cloud-only, so the Android TV Device opens the app (no single-camera deep link); the live feed still lives in Blink / on your Echo Show.
