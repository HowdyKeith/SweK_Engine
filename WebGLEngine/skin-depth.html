How far a wave gets into a conductor

In a medium with permittivity ε, permeability μ and conductivity σ, the loss tangent p = σ/(ωε) decides everything: α = ω√(με/2 · (√(1+p²) − 1)) and the skin depth is δ = 1/α, exactly.
The formula everybody quotes is a limit, and it never says so. δ = √(2/ωμσ) is the p → ∞ case. It is exact to eighteen digits for copper and 55.4% wrong at p = 1. The miss is a function of p alone — not of the material, not of the frequency, not of anything appearing in either formula.

2. The closed form grading a lossy FDTD

The window for the fit is derived from the predicted δ — half a depth in, one decade wide — not chosen by eye. A deeper window always reads a smaller α, by up to 30×. Domain length is ruled out (35 → 118 depths moves nothing) and so is the Courant number. That residue is not explained here, and the number quoted is a bound on this fixture rather than on FDTD.

3. A bisected threshold against a predicted one

FDTD's limit is S ≤ 1/√d — exact. The meter's method is to bisect.
The bisected boundary is a function of the step budget and the predicted one is not. It falls toward 1 as the budget grows and never reaches it — and it is always above the true limit, so a solver tuned to a bisected threshold is tuned just past its own cliff. Nobody could have known that before, because everywhere else the meter has been used there was no right answer to check the bisection against.

4. The edges — and the verdict on section 3's floor

a hard source is an assignment; a soft source is an accumulation
An assignment is a mirror. E[i] = f(t) overwrites the cell every step, so whatever arrives is annihilated and replaced — reflection coefficient exactly 1, not 0.99. E[i] += f(t) leaves the ordinary update in place and is exactly transparent. And Mur's first-order boundary is exact at the magic Courant number S = 1: its coefficient (cΔt−Δx)/(cΔt+Δx) vanishes and the update collapses to E0 ← E1, which is precisely what the scheme does there. That is the same fact the magic time step already rests on, arriving at the boundary.
Verdict on the unexplained floor above: partial, and named. That fixture drove its edge with an assignment, so the driven cell was a perfect mirror. One word changed: at σ = 0.01 measured/predicted falls from 1.97e+4 to 1.18 and the closed form now holds where it could not be checked before. But a residual floor near 1e-9 survives at σ = 0.02 and 0.05 — roughly five orders lower, and still open. Not retracted, no longer the same size, and no cause claimed for what is left.
