◉ KRBN HOLOGRAM — seven ghosted lumps inside the skin
booting...
Renders on RELEASE, not on drag. Measured 708 ms per frame in a real browser -- fast enough to be interactive, not fast enough for sixty a second. A slider that fires a 700 ms render on every pixel of travel is a slider that fights you.
Krbn by Valeriu Palos, MIT, vendored at /vendor/krbn with his LICENSE beside it. The skin is a VOXEL mesh from SweK's own greedy mesher -- blocky on purpose, because we have no marching cubes that emits { positions, triangles }.
