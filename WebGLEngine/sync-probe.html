Sync Walk Probe

/sync/status calls listModels(root), which walks the whole asset tree synchronously, uncached, on every hit — and both the peer radar and the settings hub poll it. On Keith's Windows rig that endpoint timed out after 4000 ms. This times that exact walk, using that exact function, and reports where the milliseconds went.
It walks twice. The first pays whatever cold cost exists; the second does not. A big gap means the cost is per-boot. A small gap means every poll pays it.
What this is allowed to conclude: almost nothing. It can see two things — time in readdir and time in stat — and each is consistent with several causes it cannot tell apart from inside the process. The outcome worth having may be the negative one: if this comes back fast, the walk is not why /sync/status timed out, the leading candidate is wrong, and the four seconds are still unexplained somewhere else.
