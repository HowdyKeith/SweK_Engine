SweK · fluid + rigid (one-way)
Click anywhere to drop a rigid body into the pool. Each body becomes a moving SOLID in the FLIP grid: the fluid's pressure solve enforces no-penetration, so the body splashes and displaces the water and the water flows around it. This is the verified 2D CPU foundation (fluid/flip2d.mjs, unit-tested: mass conserved, no particle trapped, surface displaced) for box3d rigid-fluid coupling. TWO-WAY is now live: the fluid pushes back, so light bodies (teal) float and heavy ones (amber) sink -- the float/sink threshold is at water density.
