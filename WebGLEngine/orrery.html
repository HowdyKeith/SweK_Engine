SweK ORRERY

Everything vendor/ holds, in orbit around the engine that took it in. Distance is how long ago it arrived; the period follows from that distance through physics/orbits/kepler.js, so the picture cannot drift from the physics. Colour is licence posture.

Scroll to zoom, click a body to follow it. Keep zooming and it becomes a micro planet, then its own file tree as ground.

The faint arcs are what SweK read and did not take — 39 registered sources and the 150 Khronos sample models. They are on parabolas, not orbits: nothing was captured, so nothing holds them, and they pass once and leave. How close one comes is how many files here exist because of it.
