Fleet floor atlas

A tolerance in this lab is a measured floor with 2x margin. The floor was measured on ONE machine. This page reads every bench report the fleet has submitted and asks what the margin actually is on each adapter that ran it. Not a leaderboard — speed lives in the perf ledger, and mixing them would rank a fast wrong answer above a slow right one. Float gaps (magmap, hmc-leapfrog) and the exact spin count (ising, tolerance zero) are separate metrics and are never averaged together. Gate: tools/roundhouse/floorAtlas-selfcheck.mjs.
