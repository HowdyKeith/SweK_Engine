ASCII Object

The wireframe head, rendered to a texture and sampled into a character ramp. Same module thead.html draws and the Pip-Boy screen renders — one definition, three consumers.

Ten ramp levels, not seventy. A long ramp looks impressive and quantises noise into visible bands, because adjacent glyphs stop differing in perceived weight.
Cells are twice as tall as they are wide, so the sampling grid is corrected 2:1 — the step a naive ASCII renderer skips, and the reason most come out squashed.
Transparent pixels become spaces, not the darkest glyph. A transparent background averages to zero luminance and would fill the frame with a solid block, which is the opposite of what an ASCII view is for.
