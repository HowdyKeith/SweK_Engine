◆ Rotor League

Every pilot against every other on the same scenarios. A helicopter or a quadrotor flies alone: there are no seats and no shooting, so a fight is one scenario flown by two pilots and compared by score — a win is a lead of more than the margin, anything closer is a draw. The league is a different way of counting the flights the board averages: by scenarios won, which a few lost flights with a large score cannot dominate. The rating is Bradley-Terry on the Elo scale (read differences).
