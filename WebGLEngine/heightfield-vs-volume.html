Heightfield vs Volume

Same volume, same camera, same ray, same shading. One line differs — the occupancy test. If anything else differed, the diff would be measuring the setup.
Volume march — overhangs are ordinary
Heightfield — one height per texel
Difference, amplified
The lit region is the space beneath the overhang. The heightfield does not lose the roof — the roof is the top of its column. It fills in the air underneath, because a column must have exactly one surface. You never see geometry go missing; you see solid where there should be a gap.
Control — a flat volume
The same two tracers over a volume with no overhang. They must agree exactly. A comparison that cannot come out identical is measuring its own setup, and this is the only line that proves the one above is about the projection.
