Mass vs Merit

Does the brain put its probability mass on the options that actually work? An aggregate hit rate can't answer that — a brain at 45% could be learning, or confidently choosing what fails, and those look identical in a ratio. Ranking mass (how much it favours an option) against merit (how well that option works) tells them apart.
