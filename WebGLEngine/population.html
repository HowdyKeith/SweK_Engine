Population field · readback planner

Population state lives in one float texture, one texel per agent slot. The question this page answers is the one that decides whether the design is affordable: how much has to come back across the bus to know what should spawn. Reducing to tiles on the GPU first is the difference between a multi-megabyte sync stall and a few kilobytes.

Live GPU check

Builds the real field on this machine's GPU and reports one of three outcomes. The three are kept apart on purpose: a machine that cannot do float render targets is not a broken shader, and one word for both would make every laptop look like a defect.
