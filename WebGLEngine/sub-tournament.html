◆ Sub Tournament

Every side against every other, each pair from both seats on the same geometries. A side is a tactician (the doctrine) and a motion pilot (the hand autopilot or the fruit fly network). Seat A starts with the other boat in front of its nose, and that is worth more than most of the skill in the table — so every pair is flown twice and the seat is measured.
