**The picture across H4 and H5 and H7 to H10** (the claim to carry forward, with its limits): none of the trained 963-weight networks holds a heading (the pilot's forward pass restarts from zero hidden activity every tick: the "recurrent core" is 3 rounds of message passing inside one tick, nothing carries over), the wiring does not matter, the recipe is within seed luck, the core is not where the gain is made, dark and lit gains are the same size (none of it is dropout-specific), and an 11-gain retune of the PD law inside a x8 box does not reach it. So what the networks gain over the autopilot is a generic gain from a better-tuned map from the 14 features to the four commands, found by a dive-trained search. What that map does that the PD law cannot is NOT yet known; see section 7.

## 7. What to do next (suggested, with costs; the registrations are NOT written for any of these)
1. **Channel transplant (cheap, no training; run here).** Fly each of the 28 trained networks with ONE output channel (pitch, yaw, thrust, ballast) replaced by the autopilot's own command and the other three kept: which channel carries the gain? Registered measure: the gain with each channel transplanted, per network, pooled; verdict by which single transplant removes most. 28 networks x 5 conditions x 200 ids is about 40 minutes. This is the first thing to run: every other explanation (memory, wiring, recipe, core) is now ruled out, and this is the cheapest way to say where the gain is.
2. **A wider box for the PD tune (H6 follow-up; here, about 20 minutes of training).** Gains in [default/64, default x 64] and, as a second arm, scored on the dropout's own dark heading error instead of the dive score. If a PD law with those gains reaches +0.07 the learned gain is a tuning gap after all; if it still stalls near +0.01, the network is using a nonlinearity the PD form lacks. It answers the question H6 had to leave open.
3. **Transfer (cheap; here).** Fly the 28 networks and the autopilot on the other switchboard tasks and hulls (the survey task, the Munk x3 and the other hulls of `tools/subHulls.mjs`, the flood scenario): is the +0.07 a gain on THIS task distribution only? If it vanishes off the dive distribution the networks have overfitted the dives; if it holds it is a better controller.
4. **Capacity scan (rig).** The H10 frozen-core arm with the hidden layer cut to 4, 8, 16 units (and, if wanted, widened to 68): how small can the map be and still gain? Three to four arms of 10 replicates of 4 searches each (about 8 hours of one core per arm).
5. **More replicates for H10 (rig; only if the quarter of the gain matters).** About 24 per arm to hold the +0.021 difference at 3 se.
6. **Parked from the original list:** `decision-circuit-vs-twin` (no trained circuit exists yet; needs a design step and Keith's steer), the fish pilot as a tank kind (a build-then-measure round of its own), matched controls for the other seven networks (much less pressing after H7: the seed spread is known).
