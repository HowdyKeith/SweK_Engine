* **How the task scores a crushed boat** (`brain/needsTask.mjs` `scoreOf`): `crushed ? 0 : 0.45 x stations seen + 0.45 x home + 0.1 x hull`. **So a crushed boat is NOT cheap in the score: it scores 0 against a mean near 0.82.** What is thin is the SIGNAL: the rule crushed no dive in 600 on the audit and the circuits crushed or stranded one, so a search sees a crush so rarely that it cannot learn the hull from it. The quantity the task records every dive and the score ignores is `maxStrain` (the largest hull strain, depth against the collapse depth with damage; a crush is at 1). Measured on the training, held-out and pool sets (not an audit): the rule's `maxStrain` never exceeds 0.918; the plain champion's reaches 0.952, 0.958 and 0.966 on the three sets, with 3, 15 and 16 dives above 0.9 and 1, 3 and 6 above 0.95; the no-state champion's 0.945, 0.953, 0.959. **The circuits that gained score did so partly by diving deeper than the rule ever does** (the cap output moves the cap up by as much as 0.05). A graded price on strain is therefore a signal that exists on most dives and not only on the rare crush.
* **The headroom for the station choice** (Round 25, 3 stations: the oracle's order is +0.020 above the twin, which is +0.0131 above the rule here) is about 0.033 above `rule-low06`. Round 10's learned station choice captured 6 to 25 percent of a hindsight ceiling. A learned scorer must capture more than about 60 percent of that to clear the 0.02 floor.

## 2. The questions, and what a result would and would not mean
* **S. STATION CHOICE ONLY.** If the circuit decides ONLY which station to visit next (outputs o1 to o3 of the decode; surfacing, the cap, the pump and the repair are the tuned rule's exactly), does it beat the rule (nearest-first)? the twin?
* **D. DATA.** Does a station scorer trained on 40 dives do better than the same one trained on 16 (the last round's training set)?
* **P. THE PRICE.** Trained on a fitness that charges for hull strain, does the full circuit (same net, same recipe, same seeds as last round's plain net) differ from the unpriced one in score and in how deep it dives?
* **R. REPLICATION.** Does last round's post hoc +0.0050 of the plain net over the rule hold on seeds nobody has looked at?
**A BEATS WOULD MEAN:** under this recipe on these seeds the arm is above the rule by the floor. **IT WOULD NOT MEAN:** that a circuit plans (a station score may be nearest-first with a different scale); that the fly's wiring did anything (the plain net is a plain net, no circuit is in this round); that the price is the right price (one value, fixed below); anything about other conditions or noise levels or a real boat.
