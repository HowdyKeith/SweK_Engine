## 2. The questions
* **Q1 (THE POOLED READING, per non-empty subset).** The paired difference of the score against the empty kit over the 12 scenarios: mean, se, better / worse / same (a difference within 1e-9 is the same), and the series' reading: **BEATS** iff the mean is positive, above 2 se AND at least 0.02; **IS BELOW** iff the mirror; else **TIES**. Seven readings. With twelve scenarios and the full kit's se of 0.31 on a mean of 0.22, **a BEATS needs an edge of about 0.6 and is not expected: Q1 is reported so that the table is read in the series' own words, not because a verdict is expected to fall out of it.**
* **Q2 (WHICH ONE CARRIES THE FULL KIT, the question 105 left open).** For each non-empty subset, in how many of the 12 scenarios its fight has the SAME outcome, defenders lost and score (within 1e-9) as the full kit's; and in how many it is the same as the EMPTY kit's. **The subset that matches the full kit most often is named, ties listed.**
* **Q3 (THE 105 PATTERN).** In how many of the 12 scenarios do all seven non-empty subsets score below the empty kit (105 does; the others are unflown); in how many does at least one non-empty subset score below it; in how many does at least one score above it.
* **Q4 (THE FIRST DIVERGENCE, as in 105's table).** Over the (scenario, subset) pairs with a non-empty subset, how the first divergence from the empty kit reads: a torpedo intercepted, a torpedo decoyed, a hit replaced by a different hit, a different `fire`, a loss, or none (the fight is identical to the empty kit's); counts of each, and for each subset how many of its 12 fights never diverge.
* **CHECKS (run first; a failure is the first line and the readings are NOT WEIGHED).** SCENARIOS (twelve, seeds 101 to 112 once each); SUBSETS (eight in the registered order, the same kit names); ENDS (the empty kit repeats run B and the full kit run K, by fingerprint, in every scenario: if not, the pairs flown here are not the registered read's); DETERMINISM (scenario 105's table here is the table of `brain/COUNTERFACTUAL_WIP.md` section 7: outcomes `defended`, breached x7 and the scores 1.702, -0.167, -0.467, -0.467, -0.317 x4); SCORES (finite).
