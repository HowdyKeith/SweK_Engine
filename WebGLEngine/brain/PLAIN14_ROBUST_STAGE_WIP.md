## 2. The questions
* **Q1.** Start each arm's draw from ITS OWN stage-1 champion (the dump of the same seed, same arm) and train it for the robust stage: 4 searches (seeds `52000 + 10 d + k`, d the draw's index 0 to 9, k the search 0 to 3) x 200 iterations, sigma 0.3, 1 to 4 weights a child, five vessels, **NO actuator limits** (the stage-1 champions were trained without them and this isolates the vessel randomisation; the chain's stage ran under the limits after an actuator retrain that is NOT repeated here: **this is a one-stage control, not the chain**), the whole thing under the first-order orientation step (`SWEK_6DOF_ORIENTATION=euler`) so that it matches the stage-1 environment. The champion of a draw is the search with the best robust held-out score (the chain's own rule, no eligibility test). **Then, once, on FRESH audits** (`auditSets(95000)`: the 500-dive safety audit and the 36-dive audit, seeds 95000 to 95811, nothing has used them, and the 18-dive reserve `BASELINE_SET`, as stage 1), each flown on all five vessels and averaged: the champion, its own start, and the PD autopilot.
* **Q1 WOULD MEAN:** under this one-stage objective, from these starts, the fly's wiring is (or is not) above what a net of its size with no wiring reaches. **IT WOULD NOT MEAN:** anything about the other six stages of the chain, the actuator stage, the noisy-senses stage, other hulls than these five, or other tasks; and it is a SECOND DRAW from the same twenty starts, not twenty independent chains: the arms are compared on what the stage does to each, from starts that differ only by their stage-1 luck.

## 3. Checks, run first; a failure is the first line of the report and the reading is NOT WEIGHED
**SEEDS:** ten draws of each arm, each draw once. **RECIPE:** every record carries 4 searches, 200 iterations, sigma 0.3, maxK 4, five vessels, no limits, and the first-order orientation step. **ONE ENVIRONMENT:** all twenty records carry the same autopilot numbers on the audit, the safety audit and the reserve (to 1e-9: the five-vessel mean of the hand-weights pilot flies the same dives wherever it is run). **START:** each record's recorded start-weights hash equals the hash of the champion in the stage-1 dump of the same seed and arm (the report is given the stage-1 folder and recomputes it; a draw that did not start from the stage-1 champion is a different experiment). **CHAMPIONS:** at least 8 draws of each arm have a champion (a draw in which every search failed to produce a held-out score is left out).

## 4. The reading (the safety audit, the largest set, 500 dives on five vessels; the other two reported beside it)
The same exact one-sided rank-sum as stage 1 (`tools/ship/plain14Distribution.mjs`, reused): **`THE FLY'S WIRING BEATS ITS PLAIN CONTROLS UNDER THE ROBUST OBJECTIVE`** iff the one-sided exact p that the fly is higher is at most 0.05; `IS BELOW` iff the mirror; otherwise **`THE FLY CANNOT BE TOLD APART FROM ITS PLAIN CONTROLS UNDER THE ROBUST OBJECTIVE`**. With ten against ten the test needs the fly above in at least 73 of the 100 pairs. Reported, in no verdict: the audit and reserve readings, every draw's three scores beside its own start's and the autopilot's, how many draws IMPROVED on their own start on the safety audit, the dives that ended early per arm, and the means and ranges.

## 7. The run
From `WebGLEngine\` on the rig, on this branch (PowerShell or cmd; the command sets the first-order orientation step itself, before anything imports the physics, and the four search processes inherit it):
```
node tools\ship\plain14RobustStageCmd.mjs run --all
```
It trains the twenty draws one after another, the arms alternating by seed (so a partial run is balanced), four search processes at a time, and writes `brain\rig-results\plain14-robust\robust-<fly|plain>-<S>.json` for each (plus the four search files and the start file of each draw); **a draw whose record exists is skipped, so it can be stopped and resumed**. One draw: `run --arm fly --seed 6310`. It prints each search's progress behind `[search <seed>]`. The stage-1 dumps in `brain\rig-results\plain14\` must be there (they are committed). **What comes back: the whole folder `brain\rig-results\plain14-robust`.** The reading, once, when all twenty are in: `node tools\ship\plain14RobustStageCmd.mjs report` (it reads the records and the stage-1 dumps; it trains and flies nothing).
