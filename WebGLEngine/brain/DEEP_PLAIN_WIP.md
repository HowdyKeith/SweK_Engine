* **Q1 (VERDICT 1: DEEP).** On the 1,000 deep dives, the four old searches' per-dive paired differences over the rule averaged over the four: mean, se (sample sd / sqrt(1,000)), z = mean / se. **`THE FOUR OLD PLAIN-FAMILY SEARCHES LEAD THE RULE ON DEEP DIVES`** iff z is at least 1.645; **`THE FOUR OLD PLAIN-FAMILY SEARCHES TRAIL THE RULE ON DEEP DIVES`** iff z is at most -1.645; otherwise **`THE FOUR OLD PLAIN-FAMILY SEARCHES CANNOT BE TOLD APART FROM THE RULE ON DEEP DIVES`** (also when the average has no spread at all).
* **Q2 (VERDICT 2: PLAIN).** The same on the 1,000 plain dives, with the words ending **`ON PLAIN DIVES`**.
* **Q3 (REPORTED): all sixteen by kind.** Each search's lead (se) on the deep dives and on the plain dives; by family (cx, nostate, plain, station-large, station-small, plain-priced) the mean lead and how many are above 0, for each kind. The searches share dives, so no test is given.
* **Q4 (REPORTED): the four-kind lead.** Each search's lead on far and on both from the three earlier far-and-both audits pooled (round 1's block, AUDIT 2, AUDIT 3: 1,800 dives of each kind), its lead on deep and on plain from this block, and **the mean of the four kind leads (the survey task's own weighting), with the se of that mean** (the four kinds' se combined as independent); the average of the four old searches' four-kind lead with its se and z; the six champions' four-kind leads (`cx:1, nostate:2, plain:2, station-large:2, station-small:0, plain-priced:2`). **WHAT A RESULT WOULD AND WOULD NOT MEAN.** LEAD on a kind: the old four are above the rule on that kind on dives that did not choose them; TRAIL: below; CANNOT: this block could not tell. **Round 10 already said the four are not typical of their family, so none of this is about the family; it is about what the four do on the kinds no audit flew.** A trail on deep dives would mean their far-and-both lead is paid for on a kind the rule is good at, and their four-kind lead (Q4) is the claim the survey task would make.
