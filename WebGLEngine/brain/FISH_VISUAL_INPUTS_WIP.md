## What was queried

The `datastackInfo` fields `cell_identification_table`, `proofreading_status_table` and `soma_table` are all null. **The only cell-type column is `somas.cell_type` with values `exc` (26,915), `inh` (14,510) and `na` (145,627), `classification_system` `na` throughout. There is no value that looks retinal, RGC, optic, arborization-field, tectal, periventricular, PVPN, PVIN, pretectal or isthmi.** 6,001 soma rows have root id 0.
2. **Mauthner cells: NOT labelled in the data; identified by position only (an inference, the same one the earlier fish notes made).** The earlier notes (`brain/SUBMARINE_WIP.md`) give the soma positions of the two top-ranked, mirror-paired large cells of the 30-million-row pass at v737 (562.7 / 563.7 um front-back, 157.7 / 159.3 um top-bottom, 304.6 / 222.7 um across). They receive **5,845 and 4,163** synapses (10,008 in all, the same total as the v737 export). Unchanged from the notes: they are CANDIDATES, no table or morphology here confirms them as Mauthner cells, and the 30-million-row pass was NOT re-run.
3. **Inputs at hop 1** (all 10,008 input synapses carry a sign: **8,080 excitatory, 1,928 inhibitory, 0 untagged**):
   * A: 2,039 presynaptic partners; 223 with a soma (1,119 synapses) and 1,816 without (4,726 synapses). B: 1,677 partners; 143 with a soma (516 synapses), 1,534 without (3,647). **81% of the input synapses come from fragments with no soma.** 106 partners feed both candidates (59 with a soma).
   * Soma-bearing partners: 307 distinct cells, 1,635 synapses (1,405 excitatory, 230 inhibitory). Their `cell_type`: 263 `na`, 39 `exc`, 5 `inh`. All lie in the local hindbrain: median 67 um from the candidates' soma, none further than 250 um.

4. **Retinal, structural test (inference only).** The 300 soma-less partners with the most synapses onto A and B (3,000 of the 8,373 soma-less input synapses; 3,303 soma-less partners in all) were examined through all their own synapses: 19,095 output and 577 input synapses. **85% of their output synapses lie within 100 um of the candidates' soma, none beyond 300 um; 211 of the 300 receive no synapse at all; for the median fragment 90% of its output synapses lie within 21 um of its own centre.** They are short axon pieces around the candidates' dendrites, not long tracts. That does not look like retinal ganglion cell axons arriving by an optic nerve, but it does not exclude them: 5,373 of the 8,373 soma-less input synapses were not examined, and a retinal axon that is cut into pieces by the segmentation would look like this too. Whether the optic nerve or the arborization fields are inside the volume at all was NOT checked.
5. **Tectal.** No table has tectal labels. The anatomical frame was not recoverable: the unit of `somas_distance_to_landmark.tag` and the landmark it measures are undocumented, and no table tells which end is anterior. So no count of tectal cells at hop 1 or hop 2 is given. What was counted: the inputs of the 307 soma-bearing hop-1 partners (hop 2): 88,184 synapses from 43,376 distinct sources, **95% of the synapses from soma-less fragments**, 1,003 distinct sources with a soma (779 of them new, 15 more than 250 um from the candidates, 5 synapses from a candidate itself).
6. **Both together.** Nothing in the data links a retinal class to a tectal class to the candidates, because neither class exists as a label.

## What the Escape model can use from this

* **Wired, from the data:** the two candidates plus their strongest soma-bearing partners with sign. The smallest useful set in the `giant-fiber-circuit.json` size: the 2 candidates plus the top 30 soma-bearing partners by synapses onto them (13 to 52 synapses each, 591 synapses together, signs from `synapses_axde_label`), about 32 cells. The 51 soma partners with at least 10 synapses would be the next size (not built; `vendor/zebrafish/fish1-escape-visual.json` was not written because no circuit file was asked for).
* **Not from the data:** retinal and tectal input. They enter the model as a stimulus channel, not as cells, with nothing in the data to set their number, strength or sign.

## Follow-up checks (2026-11-15, same version 759; counts from queries, inferences labelled)

**1. Circuit file.** `vendor/zebrafish/fish1-escape-top30.json`, built by `tools/fishEscapeCircuit.py` (not gated): the two candidates plus the 30 soma-bearing cells with the most synapses onto them. 32 neurons, 54 edges, 609 synapses (591 onto the candidates, 18 among the 30, none out of the candidates), every synapse signed: **500 excitatory, 109 inhibitory, 0 untagged**. Root ids are strings (they exceed 2^53). The name says "top30", not "visual": it holds no retinal or tectal cell.

**2. Are the candidates Mauthner cells? Still NOT CONFIRMED; the evidence is consistent with it and does not settle it.**
* For: a mirror pair (soma 0.6 um apart front-back, 1.6 um top-bottom, 41 um either side of the midline); the two largest synaptic inputs of any cell with a soma (5,845 and 4,163); both are hand-proofread (see 4); the input arbor and skeleton are large (dendritic cable about 1,200 and 1,800 um in the skeletons) with a lateral arm and a long arm in z (earlier notes); their skeletons reach 270 to 300 um behind the soma, which a descending axon would.
* Against or open: the skeleton service returned skeletons that are broken into pieces (286 of 404 and 256 of 545 vertices connect to the soma vertex), so no continuous axon crossing the midline and descending could be traced; the skeleton compartment labels are not usable (B has no axon label at all). Synapse size is only weakly distinctive: median box volume 96 and 62 (1e-3 um^3) against 47 for the 25 busiest soma partners, with the tails overlapping (99th percentile 3,660 / 1,830 against 1,303); the size table returned boxes for only 3,528 of the 10,008 input synapses. The earlier ranks 3 and 8 (spiral fiber or PHP, so the notes) were located (`864691128626383725`, `864691128619302817` by soma position) but their skeletons could not be fetched, so the axon-hillock test was not done.
* What would settle it: the viewer (a very large soma, lateral and ventral dendrites, a thick axon decussating and descending), or a FishExplorer / mapZebrain registration.

**3. The remaining soma-less input synapses (all 3,303 soma-less partners now, 8,373 synapses).** 106,518 output and 9,001 input synapses read. **96% of the soma-less input synapses come from fragments whose median output lies within 100 um of the candidates' soma; 3,146 of 3,303 fragments are local in that sense; only 2 have an output synapse beyond 300 um (6 synapses of 106,518) and 67 reach beyond 200 um (828 output synapses; their far ends are spread over x 337 to 820, y 106 to 420, so no single target).** 2,736 of 3,303 receive no synapse at all and 120 receive 10 or more (orphan dendrite pieces). So the soma-less inputs are overwhelmingly local axon pieces that the segmentation did not connect to a soma; there is no sign of a long tract in them. Not excluded: retinal axons cut into pieces. INFERENCE, as before.

**4. Proofreading status.** There is no proofreading table (`datastackInfo.proofreading_status_table` is null). From the chunkedgraph change log instead: both candidates are the latest root and have been edited: **A 31 merges and 1 split, B 15 merges and 4 splits** (3 user ids each); 40 of 40 random somatic cells have **zero** edits; 13 of the top 30 partners have edits (up to 21). So the candidates were hand-proofread and are not unattended segmentation; the rest of the circuit mostly is, so expect missing and wrong partners.

**5. Axon-to-axon table (`synapses_axax`, 9,539,284 rows).** Onto the candidates: 22 (A, 18 partners, 1 with a soma) and 25 (B, 24 partners, 5 with a soma) synapses; from the candidates: 11 (A, none with a soma) and 23 (B, 4 with a soma). Median 49 to 66 um from the soma, so scattered along the dendrites and axon pieces, not one cluster at the soma. The axo-axonic input expected at a Mauthner axon hillock (spiral fiber cells) is not visible as a dense cluster. Onto the 3,303 soma-less input fragments: 44,372 axax synapses onto 2,267 of them (the fragments are themselves contacted by other axons). The output side of the fragments in this table was not read (the query was stopped).

**6. Optic nerve, arborization fields, tectum in the volume: STILL NOT PLACED (2026-11-15, second attempt, with FishExplorer reachable).** `fishexplorer.zib.de` is reachable only after adding the missing intermediate certificate (GEANT TLS ECC 1, supplied by Keith, verified against the HARICA root already installed; the site sends an incomplete chain, so every client fails without it; verification stayed on). What the atlas holds: a 6 dpf zebrafish reference brain with a 142-node MECE region tree (`/sandbox/jsons/mece_wholebrain_final_test.json`) that **does name the regions asked about**: Retina, Optic Tract and Accessory Optic System, Retinal Arborization Fields AF1 to AF9 (and Clemens_AF4 to AF9), Tectum, Optic tectum, Pretectum, Preoptic Area, Rhombomere 4, Mauthner and Mauthner Cell Axon Cap, each with a 3D mesh (`lm/mece3d/<name>.zip`). **What blocks placing them in Fish1 coordinates:** (a) the meshes are not static files; the viewer fetches each through a signed URL from an application method over a websocket (the plain URLs return the web app's HTML), and the signed link points at a storage host that is not on this environment's allow-list; (b) more important, **no Fish1-to-reference-brain transform is published in anything reachable** (the Fish1 neurons are mapped into the atlas server-side by the platform, behind its sign-in, for reconstructed neurons only), so even with the meshes a region could not be put into the 8 x 8 x 30 nm voxel space of `somas` and `synapses_axde`. The atlas tutorials confirm the Mauthner cell is a named atlas region, which gives a landmark (one point, not a registration). The volume itself is described as the brain and anterior spinal cord, so the tectum, pretectum and optic tract are expected inside it, but their position and extent in Fish1 coordinates are unknown. WHAT WOULD SETTLE IT: the transform or the Fish1 positions of region masks from the Fish1 or FishExplorer authors (Petkova, Januszewski; Vohra et al. 2025), or the region meshes plus at least four shared landmarks (the Mauthner somata are one pair).
