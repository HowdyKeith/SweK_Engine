## What it is and is not
A seat that can be WATCHED in the tank. The net was trained and audited in the needs task (`brain/needsTask.mjs`, the first-order orientation step) and the tank flies the sub on its own dynamics; a survey here is a demonstration of the seat's decisions, not a score to put beside the audits. The audits found the circuit within noise of the rule (`THE CIRCUIT IS THE RULE`, `brain/DECISION_CIRCUIT_VS_TWIN_WIP.md` section 11), so the point of the menu is to watch where the two seats differ. The 600 s refusal exists because the rule's last call is the task's own clock.

## A look, POST HOC and exploratory (no test, no registration, tank scenes of seeds 1 to 30 that belong to no registered set)
Thirty tank surveys (`seed` 1 to 30, five stations, a 45 percent charge, the default gauge noise 0.1, 620 s), the rule against the circuit on the same scene: **the two fly the same survey on 16 of 30**; on the other 14 the circuit scores more on 9 and less on 5; the mean score is 0.283 for the rule and 0.313 for the circuit, **and the circuit lost 2 boats where the rule lost none** (seeds 22 and 26). No standard error was computed and none should be read into the means; the 14 differing scenes are the only information. One scene to watch, by hand, not a finding: five stations at (60,60,30) (460,80,40) (440,260,35) (80,250,30) (250,150,50), charge 0.45, exact gauge (`gaugeSd: 0`): the rule's boat is lost with none of the stations visited, the circuit's visits two. The 2 lost boats are in line with the hull cost the audit of the decision-circuit round reported (`circuit-plain` crushed or stranded one dive more than the rule).
