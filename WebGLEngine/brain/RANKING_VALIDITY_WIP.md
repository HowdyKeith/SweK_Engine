## 1. What exists, read from the files and not recalled
* **Twenty-four searches, in two training files.** `brain/decisionCircuitTraining.jsonl` (12 lines: families cx, nostate, plain, four searches each, seeds 96500 to 96523) and `brain/stationRoundTraining.jsonl` (12 lines: station-large, station-small, plain-priced, four each, seeds 98100 to 98113 and 96520 to 96523). Each line is one 4-search-family training result: `heldOut` (the mean survey score of the weights on `HELD_OUT_SET`, **40 dives, seeds 11 to 20 of the four kinds plain, deep, far, both**, noisy gauge), `poolCrashed`, the `start` it was graded against, and its weights. The same recipe in all twenty-four (4 runs, 300 children, sigma 0.3, 1 to 4 weights a child, prune budget 60) and the same start (held-out 0.88375, pool crushed 0).
* **The champion rule** (`championOf`, the decision-circuit round's, used again by the station round): ELIGIBLE = crushed in no more pool runs than the start; the champion = the best `heldOut` among the eligible, ties to the lower k; none -> the rule. **Only the champions were ever flown on an audit.** The rule never looked at an audit and the audits it was given read: the plain net +0.0050 (first audit) and +0.0066 (se 0.0034, second audit), station-large -0.0055 in the means, station-small about -0.0014, plain-priced +0.0075 against `rule-low06`. Held-out leads of those champions: plain +0.0169, plain-priced +0.0056, station-large +0.0075, station-small +0.0037. **Nobody has asked whether the held-out score, the ONLY thing the champion rule reads, ranks the searches on dives it did not see.** Round 11 of the series found held-out gains of 0.01 to 0.03 that the audits shrank to a few thousandths; this round asks the same thing of the ranking, with every search flown and not only the six winners.
* **The audit module** (`tools/ship/takeStationRound.mjs`): the survey task with the strain kept, kinds far and both, the first-order orientation step, a paired difference per dive against `rule-low06`; ~0.12 s a dive, so an arm of 1,200 dives is about 2.5 minutes of one core.

## 2. The questions
* **Q1 (THE VERDICT). Among the ELIGIBLE searches, does a search's held-out lead (heldOut minus the start's, on the UNPRICED score: `heldOutRaw` for plain-priced) rank its lead on the audit (its mean paired score minus `rule-low06`'s, same dives)?** The champion rule only ever chooses WITHIN a family, so the statistic is within families: **S = the sum over the six families, over each pair of eligible searches of the family, of sign(held-out lead i minus j) times sign(audit lead i minus j)** (a difference under 1e-9 is a tie and counts 0). There are 16 eligible searches (cx 2, nostate 3, plain 2, station-large 3, station-small 4, plain-priced 2) and **15 pairs**, 14 untied (station-small k0 and k1 have the same held-out score). The test is EXACT: the audit leads are permuted inside each family (2!x3!x2!x3!x4!x2! = 3,456 arrangements, every one counted, the observed included). **`THE HELD-OUT SCORE RANKS THE SEARCHES`** iff the one-sided p that S is as large or larger is at most 0.05; **`THE HELD-OUT SCORE RANKS THE SEARCHES BACKWARDS`** iff the p that it is as small or smaller is; otherwise **`THE HELD-OUT SCORE DOES NOT RANK THE SEARCHES`**. **THE POWER, said before the data:** computed from the held-out leads in the two files (the null distribution of S with these ties, an audit lead with no ties): S >= 8 is p 0.0483, S >= 6 is 0.1212, S = 14 is 1 in 3,456. With 14 untied pairs, 8 of the 15 must have the same order, net of the discordant ones, at an audit noise of about 0.003 per search against held-out leads that run from -0.033 (cx:0) to +0.017 (plain:2) inside the eligible: **a real but modest ranking would often read DOES NOT RANK. A DOES NOT RANK is "this round could not see it", not "there is none".**
* **Q2 (REPORTED, no verdict; each printed beside Q1).** (a) The same S pooled over all eligible searches without families (16 searches, 120 pairs) and over all 24 searches (276 pairs), p by a deterministic permutation Monte Carlo (mulberry32, seed 20261107, 1,000,000 draws; p = (1 + count) / (draws + 1)); (b) the same S WITHIN families over all 24 searches (36 pairs; the same Monte Carlo); (c) the OLS slope of the audit lead on the held-out lead over the eligible (how much of a held-out lead survives the audit) with its standard error; (d) per family, the champion's rank by audit lead among its eligible searches (1 = best) and its lead minus the mean lead of the other eligible searches of the family, with the count of families (of 6) in which the champion is above that mean; (e) the six champions' audit leads on this block beside the ones the earlier audits gave them; (f) per search: held-out lead, pool crushed, audit lead, se, better / worse / same dives.
* **Q3 (CHECKS, run first; a failure is the first line of the report and the verdicts are NOT WEIGHED).** SEARCHES (24 found, the 12 + 12 specs once each, the same start and recipe in every line); WEIGHTS (every arm flew the weights of its line, by digest); CHAMPIONS (the six champions `championOf` names carry the digests the earlier audit files record for the arms flown from them -- `brain/stationRoundAudit.jsonl` (station-large 3079e794, station-small adc0f039, plain-priced 3e0ed772, circuit-plain c916e7f5) and `brain/decisionCircuitAudit.jsonl` (the cx and nostate champions); an arm those files do not record is reported as not cross-checked, not as a failure); KEYS (every arm flew the same dives in the same order, 1,200 each); SCORES (finite, in [0, 1]); RULE (`rule-low06` flown once, from the switchboard's own arm).

## 3. The block
Seeds **19001 to 19600** of the kinds **far and both** (1,200 dives an arm, 25 arms: `rule-low06` and the 24 searches), the first-order orientation step, the noisy gauge, READ ONCE. No file of the tree reads the survey task on seeds 19001 to 19600 (checked by grep: the other uses of 19xxx are other tasks). The seeds are read only by `fly`; smoke runs use seeds 1 to 4.

**WHAT A RESULT WOULD AND WOULD NOT MEAN.** RANKS: within a family, the search with the higher held-out score tends to have the higher audit lead, so the champion rule's choice carries information. DOES NOT RANK: this round could not tell the searches apart by held-out score; it does NOT say the champion rule picks at random, nor that the six champions are no better than the rule. **IT WOULD NOT MEAN:** that any search beats the rule (that is the audit's question, and `circuit-plain` has Round 2); that held-out and audit measure the same thing (the held-out has plain and deep dives the audit does not); that the ranking holds for another recipe, another policy class or another task; anything about the searches' hull or pool behaviour beyond what the pool-crush eligibility already used. The held-out set is 40 dives and its noise is part of what is being measured.
