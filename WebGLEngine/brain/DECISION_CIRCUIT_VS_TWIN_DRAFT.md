## 1. The question, and what a result would and would not mean
**THE QUESTION.** On the survey run at the Round 33 constants, does a decision circuit that starts as the seat's own rule (D1b) decide better than (i) that rule, (ii) itself without its memory, (iii) a plain net of the same size, and (iv) the twin planner that sits on the same rule?
**WHAT A BEATS-THE-TWIN WOULD MEAN:** under this recipe, this circuit's learned residual closed more of the oracle's gap than a rollout planner on a clone of the simulator does, on these seeds. **WHAT IT WOULD NOT MEAN:** that the fly's wiring did it (the wiring controls are not the question); that a circuit plans (it may have learned a better station score); that the numbers are a real boat's (tank scale); that it holds in the other four conditions or at other noise levels; that the twin is the best planner (Round 25 says it is not).
