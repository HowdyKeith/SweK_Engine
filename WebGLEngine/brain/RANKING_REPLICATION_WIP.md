## 1. What exists, read from the files and not recalled
* **The first read** (`brain/RANKING_VALIDITY_WIP.md` section 8): over the 16 eligible searches of 24, within the six families, the 40-dive held-out lead ranked the audit lead on seeds 19001 to 19600: **S = 10 of 14 untied pairs, exact p 0.0145** (the bar was S >= 8). **That is one block, and a result that cleared a 0.05 bar is more likely to overstate its effect than to understate it.** The slope of the audit lead on the held-out lead was **0.462 (se 0.190), intercept -0.0038**: about 46 percent of a held-out lead survived. All six champions were the best eligible search of their family; only 6 of 24 searches had a positive lead over the rule and two were above 2 se (`plain:2` +0.0063, `plain-priced:2` +0.0058).
* **The held-out set is 40 dives** (`HELD_OUT_SET`: seeds 11 to 20 of the four kinds plain, deep, far, both, the noisy gauge sd 0.10). A held-out lead on 40 dives has a standard error of the order of 0.02, against leads of -0.03 to +0.017; **a bigger set measures the same quantity with less noise, which is what a slope below 1 and an intercept below 0 would be partly made of** (regression dilution; the rest would be the search's gains not carrying over).
* **The tools exist.** `tools/ship/takeRankingValidity.mjs` flies any of its 25 arms (the rule and the 24 searches) on any range and kinds (`fly --from --to --kinds`) and has the exact within-family test, the Monte Carlo, the OLS line and the champion rows; the round 1 audit file is `brain/rankingValidityAudit.jsonl`. **No new flight code is needed**; the new module reads three more files.

## 3. The questions
* **CHECKS, run first (a failure is the first line of the report and the verdicts are NOT WEIGHED).** SEARCHES (24, the specs once each, one recipe, one start); ARMS (the rule and the 24 once each in each of the three files); WEIGHTS (by digest, in all three); CHAMPIONS (the six champions are the ones the earlier audits flew); KEYS (every arm of a file flew the same dives in the same order); SCORES (finite, in [0, 1]); **H40 REPRODUCES: the mean score of each search on seeds 11 to 20 of the four kinds equals the held-out score its training line recorded (`heldOutRaw` for plain-priced) to 1e-9, and the rule's equals the start's (0.88375):** if it does not, the BIG held-out is not the quantity the champion rule used and nothing below is weighed.
* **Q1 (THE REPLICATION VERDICT).** The same exact within-family test as round 1: S over the pairs of eligible searches inside each family of sign(40-dive held-out lead i minus j) x sign(AUDIT 2 lead i minus j), exact over the 6,912 arrangements. **`THE RANKING REPLICATES`** iff the one-sided p that S is as large or larger is at most 0.05; **`THE RANKING IS REVERSED`** iff the p of S as small or smaller is; otherwise **`THE RANKING DOES NOT REPLICATE`**. (S >= 8 is p 0.0483, as before.)
* **Q2 (THE BIGGER HELD-OUT).** The same test with the BIG held-out lead against the AUDIT 2 lead: **`THE BIGGER HELD-OUT RANKS THE SEARCHES`** iff that p is at most 0.05; **`THE BIGGER HELD-OUT RANKS THEM BACKWARDS`** iff the mirror; otherwise **`THE BIGGER HELD-OUT DOES NOT RANK THE SEARCHES`**. Q2 says whether the bigger set ranks; **it does not say whether it ranks BETTER than the 40 dives, which Q3 reports without a verdict** (two statistics on 14 untied pairs cannot be told apart by a test, said before the data).
* **Q3 (REPORTED, no verdict): the two held-outs side by side on AUDIT 2**, over the eligible: S and its exact p for each, the OLS slope of the audit lead on the held-out lead with its se and the intercept for each, S pooled without families (Monte Carlo, seed 20261107, 1,000,000 draws) and within families over all 24 (exact) for each; the difference of the two S.
* **Q4 (REPORTED): the champion rule redone with the BIG held-out:** the champion of each family among its eligible searches by BIG held-out lead (ties to the lower k), against the champion by the 40-dive score; in how many families they differ; each pick's AUDIT 2 lead; the mean over the six families for each rule and their difference; in how many families the BIG pick has the higher lead, the lower, the same.
* **Q5 (REPORTED): both audit blocks together.** Each search's lead on AUDIT 2 and on round 1's block (`brain/rankingValidityAudit.jsonl`) pooled by inverse variance (2,400 dives); Q1's and Q2's test on the pooled leads; the six champions' leads on both blocks and pooled with their z.
* **Q6 (REPORTED): the hull** of the six 40-dive champions and the six BIG champions on AUDIT 2: dives above strain 0.92 and 0.95, the largest strain, the crushed or stranded dives.

**WHAT A RESULT WOULD AND WOULD NOT MEAN.** REPLICATES: the held-out score carries information about which search of a family does better on dives it did not see, on two blocks, so the champion rule's one input is worth reading. DOES NOT REPLICATE: this round could not see it again; it does not say round 1 was luck (a true effect of the size round 1 found would fail a 0.05 replication a good part of the time, which is why Q5 pools the two blocks). BIG RANKS / DOES NOT: whether 400 dives show the ranking on this block. **IT WOULD NOT MEAN** that any search beats the rule (the leads against the rule are reported, not weighed), that the ranking holds for another recipe, policy class, gauge or task, or anything about the dropped (pool-crushing) searches, which are in no verdict. Round 1's eligibility (pool crushes no more than the start's) is unchanged.
