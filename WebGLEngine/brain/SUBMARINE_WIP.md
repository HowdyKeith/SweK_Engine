## What this is NOT

* The fly's Giant Fiber Circuit is an escape reflex, not a depth controller. It is the stand-in network that tests the
  vessel, task and training loop. A result says the pipeline works, **not** that a fly (or fish) can pilot a submarine.
* **(Written before the Fish1 section at the end of this file: a real zebrafish circuit IS vendored now, and what it is and is not is said there. The paragraph below is what was true then.)** There was no fish circuit. Google/Janelia's ZAPBench (`gs://zapbench-release`, CC-BY 4.0, reachable from the

  (`PROVENANCE.md` already records that the fly circuits were fetched on the maintainer's machine). `tools/bakeConnectomeTopology.mjs`
  needs a vendored circuit JSON with `bodyId`/type/`[from,to,weight]` edges.

### Tuning the tactician (`tools/tuneSubTactics.mjs`; theta in `brain/subTacticsThetaWip.json`)

4 searches x 150 iterations, TRAIN_SET (24 duels, all four drones); champion (seed 5000) picked on HELD_OUT_SET. Score (higher is better, max 2.0):

| | TRAIN | HELD_OUT | BASELINE | BASELINE W/L/D | fresh 160 duels (instant) | fresh 160 (actuator limits) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| hand parameters | 0.998 | 1.307 | 0.815 | 14/4/6 | 1.221 (117W 11L 32D) | 1.199 |
| tuned, seed 5000 | 1.722 | 1.633 | 1.557 | 20/0/4 | 1.631 (141W 1L 18D) | 1.617 |

By drone on the 160 fresh duels: patrol 1.43 -> 1.86, quiet 0.96 -> 1.00, evader 1.57 -> 1.87, **hunter 0.92 -> 1.79**.
The tuned DOCTRINE, in plain words: **go fast and loud and shoot first** -- search at 4.1 m/s (hand: 2.0), track at 4.4 (hand 1.5), ping after the FIRST contact
and every 4 s (hand: second contact, every 12 s), fire from 447 m (hand 320) with a 30-degree aiming tolerance (hand 10; the torpedo seeker's cone is 35), evade for 31 s at 3.6 m/s.
It beats the default hunter because it shoots from further and aims less carefully, which the torpedo's wide cone forgives.

**What this does NOT show.** The opponents are FIXED scripts, not adapting: the hunter is the DEFAULT tactician, so "beats the hunter" means "beats that one doctrine". A tuned-vs-tuned
fight (self-play) has not been run. AUDIT_SET (32 duels) is unread. The 'quiet' drone (0.5 m/s, heard from ~94 m) is still about half draws: the tactician only pings AFTER a passive contact, and
no contact ever comes from a boat that quiet outside 94 m -- the policy space has no 'ping while searching' and a (1+1)-ES cannot invent one. The fix is a new parameter, not more search.

from the search-ping champion ("gen0"), each generation trained against the default and the previous three; cross-play on 24 fresh geometries (X's mean over both seats):

| league 7001 (a counter-doctrine was found) | vs default | vs gen0 | vs gen1 | vs gen2 | vs gen3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

* **The drone champion is EXPLOITABLE.** gen2 and gen3 of league 7001 beat it by 1.4-1.7 (it loses 1.15-1.43 to them). The counter-doctrine, from the theta: **stop pinging blind** (searchPing 7.7 -> 0.13 -> 0),
  because a ping is heard from ~10 km and tells the enemy where you are; **aim tighter** (aimTol 0.47 -> 0.33), **hold an echo longer** (echoWindow 13.7 -> 19.6), **evade harder and longer** (evadeTurn 1.7 -> 2.8 rad, nearly

  result; a larger league, a mixed-strategy opponent pool and a significance test over seeds are needed before saying how exploitable the doctrine is.
* No rock-paper-scissors cycle was seen (league 7001 is transitive: gen3 > gen2 > gen1 ~ gen0 > default), but with 4 generations that is weak evidence either way.

## The Sub Aquarium (UNNUMBERED; peers can send subs, and server.html shows fish/fly brain availability)

* **What it is:** `sub-aquarium.html` -- a tank (`render/subAquariumSim.mjs`) in which every sub swims flown by its OWN pilot (the PD autopilot, or the 963-weight connectome-wired network). Subs are numbers, never code.
* **Server:** `ai-bridge/subAquariumBridge.js` (+ `subAquariumCore.js`, pure) owns `/subaquarium/*` and `/ai/fishbrain/health`, `/ai/flybrain/health`. Arrive/send/seed/remove are TRUSTED-only (host, session or LAN); `from` is stamped from the connection, never the body; 64 KB, 30/min per address; the box can only send its OWN subs, and only to a LAN/mesh/loopback/.local peer, no redirects.
* **server.html:** a FLY BRAIN and a FISH BRAIN chip sit beside the GPU BRAIN chip (polled with `swekTick`), and every online peer row has a **Sub** button: it lists this box's local subs, then POSTs `{peer, id}` to `/subaquarium/send`; the peer's own trust rules decide admission. The fly chip did NOT exist before this -- only the GPU chip did -- so both were added.
* **Honest status:** the fish chip reads **stand-in** (amber) because no fish circuit is vendored (ZAPBench is activity, not wiring); it goes `live` only when `vendor/zebrafish/*.json` exists AND a pilot is wired to it. The fly chip reads live (circuit + trained flight weights present).
* **Gates:** `subAquariumCore-selfcheck`, `subAquariumBridge-selfcheck` (real loopback HTTP), `render/subAquariumSim-selfcheck`, `tools/ship/subAquariumPage-selfcheck` (headless Chromium, hostile name), `tools/ship/subAquariumServerHtml-selfcheck` (runs the chip and send code in node:vm; 21 sabotages, S11 and S21 equivalent mutants). `bridgeCensus` and `pageSections` pass; `sub-aquarium.html` is filed in UNPLACED (the drawer is Keith's call).

### Rung 3, rewritten for this circuit, and registered BEFORE any pilot is trained on it (2026-10-24)

#### Rung 4a -- the task's validity check, run on the AUTOPILOT ALONE (2026-10-24; a throwaway prototype of the dropout wrapper, no circuit touched)

#### Rung 4a -- the REFERENCE ARM: a hand-written dead-reckoning navigator (added at Keith's request, 2026-10-25, BEFORE it is built or run and BEFORE rung 4a's stage A has started: `brain/flyCxStageA.jsonl` does not exist yet)
**Why.** The registration calls the dropout task's headroom large ("a stateful pilot that integrates the yaw rate has a lot of headroom"). That is a claim about what a navigator that integrates the gyro could do, and nothing has measured it. A circuit's score on this task can only be read against what a plain integrator achieves.
**The arm (`tools/subNavigator.mjs`, to be built and gated; a pure wrapper, no training, no weights).** It sits between the dropout and the autopilot: `dropout(navigator(autopilot))`. While the compass is valid it passes the world through and remembers the true heading and the heading rate. While it is dark it advances its heading estimate by the HEADING RATE of the vessel, computed exactly from quantities that stay true in the dropout (the body rates and the pitch and bank): for a nose direction f and an angular velocity omega in the world frame, d(heading)/dt = -(omega_z - f_z (f . omega)) / (f_x^2 + f_y^2), by the trapezoid rule over the 30 Hz ticks, from the last valid tick. It then turns the presented world (orientation, velocity, current) about the vertical so the autopilot reads the ESTIMATED heading instead of the held one. A new dive (the target object changing) resets it. It reads no other sense than the pilots do (the same 14 features' sources: attitude, body rates, depth, speed), has NO noise on the gyro (the simulator's body rates are exact, and the arm is said to be a CEILING for a navigator with a perfect gyro, not a model of a real one), and has no knowledge of the windows.
**What is measured, once, with nothing selected.** On the audit sets of rung 4a (`auditSets(30000)`: the 36-dive audit, the 500-dive safety audit, the reserve) and with the same windows as every circuit meets (the generator is seeded by dive number), three autopilots: CLEAN (no dropout), DROPOUT (the autopilot as the study report prints it), and NAVIGATOR (this arm through the dropout). Plus the mechanism number: the mean heading error inside the dark windows for the NAVIGATOR arm. The arm is not a champion and nothing is chosen on these numbers, so reading the audit sets for it does not spend them for any circuit.
**The derived number: fraction of the gap closed**, per audit, G = (score - DROPOUT) / (CLEAN - DROPOUT), for the NAVIGATOR arm, for the cx champion and for each family's mean when the stage A results file is given. NOT in the verdict; printed beside it.
**What it will be read as, fixed now:** (1) if G of the NAVIGATOR arm is at least 0.5 on all three audits, a gyro integral recovers most of what the dropout costs, the headroom is real, and a circuit is to be read against it (a cx champion with G below the arm's did less than an integral does, whatever the verdict says about its wiring). (2) If G is below 0.5 on any audit, a plain gyro integral does NOT recover most of the cost on this task, the headroom in the registration was overstated, and every statement about what a circuit "could" do on it is withdrawn. (3) Either way nothing is claimed about the fly: the arm says what the TASK allows, not what a wiring does.
**What it is not:** a new rung, a control of the verdict, or a pilot to ship. It uses the dropout's own presentation of the world, so a navigator that cheats through that presentation would show G near 1 for the wrong reason; the gate holds that the arm reads only what the study pilots read.

### RUNG 4a, VERSION 2 -- THE ESTIMATOR: the same reservoirs, a regression readout, and a target the simulator knows (REGISTERED 2026-10-27, BEFORE anything is built or run; Keith: "lets do as you planned out")
**Why.** Rung 4a (above) failed under a recipe whose memory gain it could not reach and whose training signal was a dive score. It is cheap (minutes a circuit, no search) and it can say something the first could not: whether the real wiring is a better reservoir than other wirings, AND whether any of them can integrate the yaw rate at all.
**The target.** delta(t) = the true heading minus the heading the dropout shows (0 whenever the compass is valid; during a window it grows as the vessel turns). It is exactly what `tools/subNavigator.mjs` computes and applies by `turnWorld`; the reference arm's value for it is the kinematic integral.
**The estimator's senses, FIXED NOW to stop a leak found while writing this.** The pilots' 15 features, EXCEPT feature 4 (the heading error, which is the target heading minus the shown heading): in a well-flown dive the true heading is near the target, so feature 4 is nearly delta and a stateless readout would score well offline and fail in the loop (it would turn the shown heading onto the target and the vessel would stop steering). Feature 4 is set to 0; everything else is what a pilot reads (the yaw rate, pitch, bank, sideslip, speed, depth error, the trims and the compass flag). Said as a limit: the other features still correlate with delta through the dive's own manoeuvres.
**The data.** Dives flown by the autopilot behind the ORACLE navigator (`dropout(navigator(autopilot))`, so the vessel flies as if the heading were known), the features recorded from the world the dropout PRESENTS and delta from the true heading, tick by tick, with the default windows (two of 16 s). **The reservoir.** `brain/pilotSubCircuitState.mjs` UNCHANGED (its `update`, the fixed signed count matrix scaled to singular value 1, the seeded input weights, the 88 input cells): h' = relu(b + W_in x + sigma M h), reset at the start of a dive. **sigma is swept, not searched: {0.95, 0.99, 0.999}.** The readout is **ridge regression** from the 64 output cells' activity plus a constant to delta, over every tick of the fitting dives, with the ridge penalty lambda in {1e-6, 1e-4, 1e-2, 1}. This is a mechanism that fits what was measured, not a test of it.
**What the result says, and does not.** The flag was not the whole of v4's failure: with the flag removed and the registered side coding the input still carries nothing a linear readout can use, and the cause found is that the graph, read as signed synapse counts, cannot tell a left turn from a right one. The v3 conclusion survives every input tried (random mix, flag-and-rate ring map, flag-free ring map, unsigned rate).

'I expect RMF to read close to RMU in the controls': wrong in direction, RMU is far above RMF. 'A PASS under one in five': no PASS.
**Limits.** Not tested: the glomerulus-level shift (the dominant PEN-to-EPG offset is -1 glomerulus on both sides in the instance names; whether the two sides map to opposite ring directions depends on how the hemispheres are numbered, which was not resolved), a sign-sensitive readout, a decoder of bump position, an initial heading cue. **What a further attempt would need to be a new registration, not an edit of this one:** a map that uses the glomerulus number of each PEN and EPG cell (so that a turn to the right drives a shifted subset of the EPG cells and a turn to the left the opposite subset), or a readout that can tell the two PEN populations apart, with the pass/fail written first. This note proposes nothing more and starts nothing.
