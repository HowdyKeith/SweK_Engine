## 1. The question, and what a result would and would not mean
**THE QUESTION.** On the survey run at the Round 33 constants (noisy gauge, kinds `far` and `both`), does a decision circuit that starts as the seat's own rule decide better than (i) that rule, (ii) itself without its memory, (iii) a plain net of the same size, and (iv) the twin planner that sits on the same rule?
**A BEATS-THE-TWIN WOULD MEAN:** under this recipe, this circuit's learned residual closed more of the oracle's gap than a rollout planner on a clone of the simulator does, on these seeds. **IT WOULD NOT MEAN:** that the fly's wiring did it (the wiring controls are not run); that a circuit plans (it may have learned a better station score); that the numbers are a real boat's (tank scale); that it holds in the other four conditions or at other noise levels; that the twin is the best planner (Round 25 says it is not, by 0.020 to 0.031 at 3 to 5 stations).

## 2. What the series already says (the prior, from the draft's reading of the notes)
Round 6: a learned residual in the advisor seat beat its start by 0.11 on every audit and tied its shuffled control. Round 7: "tuning explains it" -- a constant threshold shift of 0.07 does what the residual does. Round 10: a learned station choice on the tuned rule does not beat nearest-first and captured 6 to 25 percent of a hindsight ceiling. Round 12/22/33: the twin's gain over the tuned rule is +0.012 to +0.033, and Round 25: the oracle's order is a further +0.020 / +0.028 / +0.031 above it at 3 / 4 / 5 stations. **So the prior for a decision residual is that it finds the retuning, not the planning; the only place a circuit can beat the twin is the station choice (outputs o1 to o3), and it has to close more than half of a gap that Round 10's net closed 6 to 25 percent of.**

## 4. Checks, run first and recorded; a failure is the first line of the report and the verdicts below it are NOT WEIGHED
**START:** the untrained `circuit-cx`, `circuit-nostate` and `circuit-plain` each equal `rule-low06` dive for dive (scores and fingerprints) on 24 baseline dives. **KEYS:** every arm flew the same scenarios in the same order. **CALIBRATION** (a line that cannot fail the run): `twin-standard` minus `rule-low06` on this block, to be read against +0.012 to +0.033. **TRAINING LEDGER:** how many searches of each family had an eligible champion, how many weights moved from the start and by how much, the last training score. A family with no eligible champion is `rule-low06` and is reported as that.

## 5. The verdicts (pooled paired differences over the 600 dives)
A pair reads **BEATS** iff the mean paired difference is positive, above 2 se AND at least 0.02 (the series' floor, `MIN_EDGE`); **IS BELOW** iff the mirror; else **TIES**.
* **V1** `circuit-cx` against `rule-low06`.
* **V2** `circuit-cx` against `circuit-nostate`, and against `circuit-plain`: the memory and the reservoir, one line each.
* **V3** `circuit-cx` against `twin-standard`.
* **V4 (the hull)**: crushed plus stranded dives of `circuit-cx` against `rule-low06`'s by Round 33's test (b - b0 <= 2 sqrt(b + b0)): NO WORSE / PAYS MORE.
* **BOTTOM LINE, the first that applies:** (1) V4 PAYS MORE -> `THE CIRCUIT BUYS ITS SCORE WITH THE HULL`. (2) V1 TIES or IS BELOW -> `THE CIRCUIT IS THE RULE`. (3) V1 BEATS and V3 IS BELOW -> `THE CIRCUIT BEATS ITS RULE AND NOT THE PLANNER` (and V2 TIES against both controls adds `...AND NEITHER ITS MEMORY NOR ITS RESERVOIR IS WHAT DID IT`). (4) V1 BEATS and V3 TIES -> `THE CIRCUIT AND THE PLANNER CANNOT BE TOLD APART`, stated with the power: 600 dives resolve a difference of about 2 se. (5) V1 BEATS and V3 BEATS -> `THE CIRCUIT BEATS THE PLANNER`, with V2 and V4 in the same line.
