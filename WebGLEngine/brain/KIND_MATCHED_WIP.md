## 3. The questions
* **CHECKS, run first (a failure is the first line of the report and the verdicts are NOT WEIGHED).** SEARCHES (24 specs once each, one recipe, one start); CHAMPIONS (the six are the ones the earlier audits flew); ARMS, WEIGHTS (by digest), KEYS and SCORES in all seven files; BLOCKS (each file flew the registered range and kinds: a smoke file is named as one); DISJOINT (no dive is in two of the seven files); H40 REPRODUCES (the 40-dive file re-flown equals the training lines' to 1e-9, as in round 8).
* **Q1 (THE VERDICT).** The exact within-family test of round 1 and round 8: S over the pairs of eligible searches inside each family of sign(MATCHED lead i minus j) x sign(TARGET lead i minus j), exact over the 6,912 arrangements. **`THE KIND-MATCHED HELD-OUT RANKS THE SEARCHES`** iff the one-sided p that S is as large or larger is at most 0.05; **`THE KIND-MATCHED HELD-OUT RANKS THEM BACKWARDS`** iff the mirror p is at most 0.05; otherwise **`THE KIND-MATCHED HELD-OUT DOES NOT RANK THE SEARCHES`**. Ties within 1e-9 count 0. This is the only verdict of the round; Q2 to Q7 are reported and carry no word that is a verdict.
* **Q2 (REPORTED, with the words of the same test): MATCHED beside MIXED, each against TARGET and against AUDIT 3 alone,** over the eligible: S and its exact p, the OLS slope of the audit lead on the held-out lead with se and intercept, S within families over all 24 (exact); the difference S(MATCHED) minus S(MIXED); and a **paired bootstrap of that difference over seeds** (the 150 MIXED seeds and the 300 MATCHED seeds resampled with replacement, the same resampled seeds for every search, 2,000 draws, seed 20261108, TARGET held fixed): the mean, the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles and the share of draws above 0. It reads whether the difference is bigger than the dives' own noise; with 15 pairs a reading has little power and **a difference the interval contains 0 for is not evidence of equality.**
* **Q3 (REPORTED): the champion rule redone with each held-out:** the champion of each family among its eligible searches by MATCHED lead (ties to the lower k), by MIXED lead, and the 40-dive champion of the training files; the TARGET lead and the AUDIT 3 lead of each pick; in how many families the picks differ; the mean over the six families for each rule; **and the best an oracle could do** (the eligible search with the highest TARGET lead of each family) and each rule's gap to it.
* **Q4 (REPORTED): how well the audits agree** (the ceiling of any held-out): S and exact p over the 16 eligible, within families, for round 1 against AUDIT 2, round 1 against AUDIT 3 and AUDIT 2 against AUDIT 3; and the standard deviation of the TARGET leads over the eligible against the mean se of one audit's lead.
* **Q5 (REPORTED): the earlier held-outs against the new audit.** The 40-dive set (the original, the one the champion rule reads), the BIG 400 dives and the BIG far-and-both 200 dives (round 8's post hoc cut), each against AUDIT 3 alone (fresh for all three) with S and exact p, and against AUDIT 2 and round 1's block (where the post hoc cut was found) so that the numbers of section 1 are recomputed by the committed reader.
* **Q6 (REPORTED): which kind carries it.** On MIXED, the held-out lead from the plain dives alone (150), the deep alone, the far alone, the both alone, each against TARGET: S and exact p.
* **Q7 (REPORTED): the hull** on AUDIT 3 of the six 40-dive champions and of the MATCHED picks (dives above strain 0.92 and 0.95, the largest strain, crushed, stranded), beside the rule's.

**WHAT A RESULT WOULD AND WOULD NOT MEAN.** RANKS: a held-out of 600 far-and-both dives carries information about which search of a family does better on far-and-both dives it did not see, on blocks that did not choose the cut. It says nothing about plain or deep dives, it does not make the shipped champions better (the training commands read the 40-dive set and are not changed here), and it is a statement about these 16 searches of these six families and this one start. DOES NOT RANK: surprising given the audits' agreement and the noise model of section 1; it would say that the post hoc cut was a feature of the blocks it was found on. BACKWARDS would be a finding about the task. **Q2's bootstrap is the only reading that speaks to MATCHED against MIXED and it is reported, not a verdict; the round does not claim the matched set is better unless the interval says so and Q1 reads RANKS.** About twenty readings are printed; one is a verdict, and any other that looks good is read as one of about twenty.
