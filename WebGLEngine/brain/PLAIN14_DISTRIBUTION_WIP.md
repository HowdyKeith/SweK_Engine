## 3. Checks, run first; a failure is the first line of the report and the reading is NOT WEIGHED

## 4. The reading (the safety audit, the largest set, 500 dives; the other two are reported beside it)
An exact one-sided rank-sum (Mann-Whitney) test of the fly's champions against the plain champions: **`THE FLY'S WIRING BEATS ITS PLAIN CONTROLS`** iff the one-sided exact p that the fly is higher is at most 0.05; **`THE FLY'S WIRING IS BELOW ITS PLAIN CONTROLS`** iff the mirror; otherwise **`THE FLY CANNOT BE TOLD APART FROM ITS PLAIN CONTROLS`**. Reported, in no verdict: the audit and reserve p-values, every draw's three scores, the means, the difference of the means, the ranges, how many draws QUALIFY (the trainer's own test: better than the autopilot on the held-out, audit and safety audit, no dive ended early that the autopilot completes), and the committed draws' history (not in the test).
