Waveguide cutoffs

Below its cutoff a hollow pipe carries nothing. Above it, a discrete set of modes, all from one 2D eigenproblem on the cross-section. TE and TM differ only in a boundary condition — Ez must vanish on a conductor (Dirichlet, so there is no TM10), Hz has a vanishing normal derivative (Neumann, so TE10 exists and is what every guide is designed around).
