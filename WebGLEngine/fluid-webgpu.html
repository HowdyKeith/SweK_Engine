SweK · FLIP/PIC fluid WebGPU
Hybrid FLIP/PIC on a staggered MAC grid: atomic particle→grid splat, Jacobi pressure projection (incompressibility), grid→particle FLIP/PIC blend, RK2 advection. Algorithm mirrors fluid/flip2d.mjs, whose physics is unit-tested (divergence collapse, settling, mass conservation). ST-FLIP (SIGGRAPH 2026) large-time-step spacetime sampling is toggleable (motion-blurs the P2G deposit across the time-slab). A red-black Gauss-Seidel + SOR solver is also selectable.
