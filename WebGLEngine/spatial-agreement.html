Spatial agreement

This engine holds five independent answers to one question — which bodies are near which. Each is gated against its own brute force, in isolation; none referenced another. They never met, and the reason is not neglect: they answer two different questions wearing similar names.

The point family — an L2 sphere test

k-d tree, molecular-dynamics neighbour grid, SPH spatial grid. Same question, so these must agree with brute force exactly — no superset slack.

The box family — an L∞ box test

A uniform AABB grid and a balanced BVH, sharing no code. Give each point a box of half-extent r/2 and overlap means Chebyshev ≤ r.

Across the families

Since |d|2 ≤ r implies |d|∞ ≤ r, sphere pairs are a subset of box pairs — exact geometry, no tolerance. And they must not be equal: if they ever matched, one family would be computing the other's metric and every agreement above would be vacuous. Agreement is the failure mode here, not the success.
