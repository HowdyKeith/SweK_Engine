Ragdoll

A rig is a joint graph. Shoulders and hips are spherical, elbows and knees are revolute, the spine is a weld.

This build cannot do joints

What this is

Fourteen boxes and thirteen joints. Box3D compiled all eight joint types from its first build; the SweK shim bound none of them until v2515, so the engine had rigid bodies and no way to attach one to another.

The joints are solved inside Box3D, so they inherit its determinism and cost lockstep nothing. What would cost lockstep everything is whatever drives them — which is why the shove below is fired on a tick index and not on a timer. Two machines running this from the same seed step the same ragdoll.

Determinism

The hash below is Box3D's own state hash over every body's transform and velocity. Run this on two machines and compare: identical hashes at the same tick mean the rigs are bit-for-bit the same, which is the precondition for lockstep. Different hashes mean stay owner-authoritative.
