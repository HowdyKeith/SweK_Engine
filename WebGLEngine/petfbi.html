Send email to admins

Draft = open your mail app (mailto). Gmail SMTP = send directly (needs a Gmail app password, not your normal password). Outlook = send via the installed Outlook (no credentials — uses your configured profile). Stored in ~/.voxelbridge/ (never packaged).

Outlook
