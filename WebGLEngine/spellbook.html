THE SPELL BOOK

Every cost here is derived, not typed. A spell's price is the work it actually does — particles built, voxels fractured, frames ray-marched — times a unit price measured by doing that work. There is no cost: field anywhere in the book. Make a spell cheaper to render and it becomes cheaper to cast, with nothing edited.
