CSG — solid modelling with dual contouring

Primitives combined by boolean operations into an exact signed distance field, contoured live. The mesh is built by physics/mesh/dualContour.mjs from Hermite data — a crossing point and an analytic normal on every grid edge — so sharp corners land where they actually are instead of being cut off. Toggle mass-point to see what a naive implementation does to the same field.

Drag to rotate. Wireframe of the contoured quads; the corner marker is the exact analytic corner, so any gap between it and the mesh is the error.
