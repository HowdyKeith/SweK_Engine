📥 App installer & links
Install links for the apps this engine talks to — handy on the Shield, where you sideload instead of using Google Play. The Shield has no browser by default; the usual flow is Downloader by AFTVnews → paste an APKMirror link → install. Or skip that: drop .apk files into ai-bridge/apks/ on the PC and push-install them straight to the Shield over ADB below. For Home Assistant on the TV itself, QuickBars (top of the list below) is the TV-native overlay \u2014 install from Google Play on the Shield, or push its APK over ADB, then launch it by package dev.trooped.tvquickbars.
