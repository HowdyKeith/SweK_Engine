The consistency board

Every other gate in this lab holds a measurement against algebra or a sabotaged twin. This board holds measurements against EACH OTHER: one constant, two routes that share no mechanism, and a verdict on their agreement. The admission rule: a susceptibility amplitude-ratio and a Binder cumulant weigh different moments; a Jarzynski average and a Crooks fit read different features of the work distribution; a configured viscosity is an input while a shear-decay rate is behaviour. Two seeds of the same estimator do not qualify and never will. Gate: physics/consistency-selfcheck.mjs. Pairs will grow — T_c already has a third candidate route (the specific-heat scaling) waiting on statistics.
