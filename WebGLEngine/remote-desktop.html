Screen sharing over a media-only peer connection whose SDP and ICE ride the data channel cloudPeer already opened. No new signalling path, no new token, and on a LAN the video never leaves the wire. View only — a browser cannot inject OS input, so control needs a native helper that isn't installed here, and this page will not pretend otherwise.
paints a known pattern, reads it back, and reports two numbers instead of a picture
Why two numbers. Fidelity alone ranks a frozen stream above a damaged one — a freeze scores about 0.98 while a blanked region scores about 0.81. The frame counter is painted into the image as binary, so a dead stream shows up as latency where fidelity saw nothing. Neither number is sufficient; together they separate working, stalled, and torn.
