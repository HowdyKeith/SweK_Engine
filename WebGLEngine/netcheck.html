📡 Network Diagnostic — peer reachability & AP isolation

Checks every SweK peer this box knows about. For each one it asks two things: do we hear its beacon? (UDP broadcast auto-discovery) and can we actually reach it over HTTP? The signature of AP / client isolation is a peer you hear but cannot reach — the router passes broadcast but blocks device-to-device traffic. That's the usual cause of “detects an update but never receives it.”

What the statuses mean:
• reachable — healthy; this peer can send/receive updates and streams.
• isolated — we hear its beacon but can't reach its HTTP. Almost always AP / client isolation on the router (or 2.4/5 GHz band separation). Fix: turn OFF AP isolation / client isolation / wireless isolation for the main network, and put both boxes on one SSID/band. If you can't, use Tailscale/ZeroTier — the VPN overlay bypasses isolation.
• no beacon — reachable over HTTP but we don't hear its UDP beacon. Broadcast is being dropped (common on some Wi-Fi), but the saved roster still lets updates flow — usually fine.
• offline — neither beacon nor HTTP. The box is off, on a different subnet, or fully firewalled.
