Krbn × Lyapunov

Krbn drawn to pencil SVG, rasterised, and lit by swk_lyapunov — every pixel iterating x ← r·x(1−x) for itself. Bright is chaotic, dark is a periodic window. At r = 4 the exponent is exactly ln 2 = 0.6931472, an answer the shader is never given. Press “read ln 2 off your GPU” and this page decodes it back off the canvas in front of you — it does not quote the gate's number, it takes its own.
