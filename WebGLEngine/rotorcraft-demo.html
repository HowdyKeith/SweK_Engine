◆ Rotorcraft Demo

A helicopter or a quadrotor flown by a hand autopilot and by a trained pilot on the same scenario, replayed side by side as a plan view and a side view. Turn the gusts up on a hover; take the engine or a motor away and watch how each one comes down. The machines are drawn at a fixed size in pixels, not to scale. On the board's 48 and 96 fresh scenarios the trained pilots tie the hand pilot at flying; the helicopter's gain is in the engine-out landings, and the quadrotor survives no motor failure but for one MLP in 24.
