magmap kernel — A/B bench

The kernel has never been tuned: workgroup_size(64) was chosen once, and the inner loop reads the trig tables from global storage 2304 times per thread. This page times variants of both on this device's GPU, because the machine that wrote them has none.

Every variant is bit-identical by construction — they change only where numbers are read from and how threads are grouped, never the arithmetic. This page verifies that on your device before it reports a time: a variant that is faster but disagrees with the reference is marked REJECTED, not celebrated.
