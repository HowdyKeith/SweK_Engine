🌊 WebGL2 Water — heightfield ripples, differential-area caustics, Fresnel surface

A raw-WebGL2 port of the classic technique (Evan Wallace / jeantimex): a 2D wave equation solved on the GPU with ping-pong float textures, caustics cast on the floor by the differential-area method, and a surface that blends refraction and reflection by Schlick's Fresnel. The math is proven in water/tools/water-selfcheck.mjs; this is the render.
